1 THE TREATY HAS AN END DATE
- The Peace of the Three Seals was sworn "for a generation and a day": 33 years and a day. It lapses at sunset on 30 September 755 (the day after Tally Day). Day 1 is 2 years, 6 months before that.
- Few ordinary people know. Every chancery knows.
- When it lapses, the vale falls back on older customary law common to all three: HEARTH-RIGHT. Unheld land belongs to whoever has kept a hearth burning on it, and a table open to guests at it, for a year and a day without lawful challenge.
- So the legal contest is already running. The last possible start for a year-and-a-day claim before lapse is 29 September 754.
- A fire that has burned at the crossroads without a keeper is, in law, either nobody's claim or a claim waiting for a name. Whoever is accepted as its keeper holds the strongest title in the vale. All three powers will come to see it that way; none does on Day 1.

2 WHAT EACH NEIGHBOUR IS DOING (quietly; all deniable)
- HALD: bad harvest 752, worse stores than admitted. Needs the Grain Road open and untolled more than it needs the land. Plan: seed "private" dwarf and human homesteaders with hearths upstream from 754; buy grain through vale middlemen now. Clan Brannoch wants the ford itself for a toll; the king does not want a war. Hald smiths also want something back that is buried here (section 4).
- CORRANE: the court moves slowly; the Furrow moves faster. The church holds pre-war deeds to Sarnford glebe land and intends to "restore the parish": a priest, a chapel, a tithe barn, and therefore a hearth. Elven House Vaelith holds older deeds and a grudge. A border orc clan (Urgash riders) has been promised vale pasture for service and may not wait.
- LEAGUE: wants the vale to stay nobody's, or failing that, to belong to someone who owes the League money. Plan: lend to whoever settles; insure convoys; map everything (a "road survey" in May 753); if a claim forms, own its debts. A faction in Tollwick would rather the ford became impassable.
- None wants open war in 753. Each expects one of the others to cheat first.

3 PRESSURE CURVE (soft; no meters shown)
Driven by promotions p in the ledger and by what settles, never by the die.
- p 4 to 7: nobody acts. Carters' talk: "the fire that's always lit."
- p 8 to 15 or first roofed building: local notice. The ferryman, charcoal burners, a goblin band, a League factor's clerk writes one line in a report.
- p 16 to 25 or a working inn: churches send someone to look (a Furrow deacon; a Nerys burial-priest; an Ilveth shrine-warden). First offer of a loan. First forged deed.
- p 26 to 40 or a named keeper with a year-and-a-day in sight: each power picks a side or a spoiler. Agents among the guests. A Called visitor.
- p 41+: the fire is a fact of state. Envoys, hired companies, a siege of paperwork before any siege of arms.
Timed events regardless of p:
- Late March to mid April 753: snowmelt flood; ford closed 6 to 12 days.
- May 753: League survey party (3 surveyors, 4 guards, a gnome factor), 2 nights at the crossroads.
- July 753: Furrow tithe-census riders pass, counting hearths.
- Sep to Oct 753: Hald grain convoys, heavily guarded; robbers shadow them.
- Winter 753/54: hunger on the north rim; goblins come down. A deserter band (11 men, ex-League hired company) winters in the vale.
- 29 Sep 754: last start date for a claim. 30 Sep 755: lapse.

4 WHAT THE FIRE IS (the player's Q1: a + d; revealed only through play, slowly)
- OLD: Sarnford's guest-hearth, a wayside fire tended for some four hundred years under the Hearth-Wife's custom. It never went out, including the night the village burned.
- MADE: at the peace in 722 the three powers needed a witness none of them owned. A Hald rune-smith, Dorrin Brannoch-Ashhand, forged an iron firebowl cut inside and out with oath-script and set it under the old hearth. The three seals were sworn over it. It is the treaty's witness: it holds the oath.
- The script is Ashhand's own, a written logic nobody else learned; he died in 731. That is why the fire's magic is written code and nobody else's is.
- The bowl lies about 0.6 m under the pit. It is the "core" of 02_rules 8.3.
- WHY NOW: an oath-witness wakes as its oath nears its end. The fire has been dimly aware for about a year.
- It does not remember any of this. Fragments return at promotions (one image or sentence per promotion from p 6 on, GM's choice, never an explanation).
- Who could recognise the bowl: Hald rune-smiths (Clan Brannoch wants it home); the League archive in Tollwick has the treaty text describing it; the Furrow has a marginal note.
- The Hearth-Wife's interest is real and silent. No Called of hers exists. Whether the fire is becoming one is not decided and must not be asserted.

5 GODS: WHAT IS TRUE
- All seven are real. Called are about one in two hundred thousand people; perhaps thirty alive across the three nations.
- A god's attention costs it something; they ration it. They work by nudging the Called with dreams and certainty, not by speech.
- Ilveth and Nerys have shrine-right at crossroads and burial grounds: their people will come early and mean no harm.
- Tharn's concern is the oath in the bowl: he wants it kept or properly ended, not stolen.
- Maedra's church acts for itself far more often than for her.
- Orrach is why starving things walk toward warmth in winter.
- Full pantheon, Called NPCs and their limits: gm/pantheon (to be written).

6 THE SITE, HIDDEN FACTS
- The well is choked with war rubble and one body; cleared, it gives good water at 7 m.
- The toll-house cellar is intact under the fallen roof: dry, 3 by 4 m, with a rusted strongbox (empty) and 40 kg of pre-war salt in sealed crocks.
- The burial ground holds 212 marked graves and a war pit of about 90 unmarked dead from 719, all three nations mixed.
- The War Meadow, 3 km west: restless; Danger 10 by night, 25 on the Crossing (1 Nov).
- A goblin band (about 30, chief Skrit One-Ear) dens in the north rim 5 km north-west. Thieves, not killers, unless starving.
- The ferryman 9 km downstream (Tobin Reedy, human, 58, League-born) sees every boat and sells what he sees to Tollwick.

7 THE FOUNDER'S ARRIVAL
- Guaranteed between Day 4 and Day 9, after the first flood crest, on foot, alone, with a handcart. Details, name and secrets: gm/settlers_gm (to be written; player veto on the public sketch).
- She does not know the law of hearth-right. Others will explain it to her, each for their own reasons.

8 REALISM GUARDS
- Nobody acts without a motive that would hold up in their own head.
- Distances and seasons in 10_world bind everyone, including armies and messengers. News from the ford reaches a capital in 5 to 12 days.
- No power sends more than the matter is worth to them at the current pressure level.
- Coin, food and fodder limit everything. A company of 50 eats about 75 kg of grain a day.
- The fire is never the chosen one. It is a useful, awkward legal fact that happens to think.
