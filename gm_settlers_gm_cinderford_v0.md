1 HIDDEN SETTLER MACHINERY
1.1 Will they stay? Never rolled as a favour. A PASSING guest becomes STAYING only if at least two are true: they have a reason not to go home; there is work or safety here they lack elsewhere; someone here matters to them; the place gave them something unasked. STAYING becomes SETTLED only when they do something that costs them (spend savings, send for kin, burn a bridge, bury someone here).
1.2 Leaving: each deep settler has one stated BREAKING POINT. If it happens and nothing repairs it within a week, they go.
1.3 Away (Q31 b): when a settler leaves on business, set in private: errand, distance (10_world travel speeds), Danger (0/10/25/40), stake. Resolve with one hidden d100 against their own skill base at the moment they would return, bands per 02_rules 5.3. Outcomes are real: coin, goods, wounds (Thornwall health levels), news, a companion, a pursuer, or death. They return on the day distance allows, never early for convenience.
1.4 Their account: each tells it their own way. Note what they leave out.
1.5 Awareness of the fire (Q32 b): private scale per settler: 0 a fire · 1 an odd fire · 2 something tends it · 3 it listens · 4 it acts · 5 it thinks and can be dealt with. Moves only on evidence they witness (a cast heard in the crackle, a wisp, Gary, an ember that sees). Curious or trained people test it; test = their Insight-type base vs the fire's Veil, hidden. Each step up changes how they talk about it and to whom.
1.6 Settler skill bases use the same ladder (20/30/45/60/75/85/95) in 3 to 5 named skills each. Unlisted skill = 20.
1.7 Guest-right: breaking it at this fire is remembered across the vale within a month and costs the breaker dearly with ordinary people.

2 THE FOUNDER
NAME: BRAKKA TOLWEN. Half-orc, 44. Born on a League grain barge.
2.1 Family
- Father: Urgash-clan orc farrier in Corrane border service, Dhurek; left the clan for her mother; dead 12 years. The Urgash count Brakka as kin whether she likes it or not.
- Mother: Henna Tolwen, human, League barge cook; alive, 68, in Tollwick, sharp as ever. Brakka writes twice a year.
- Daughter: Ysolt Tolwen, 19, quarter-orc, apprenticed to a Tollwick dyer (tiefling house Marrowmere). They have not spoken since Brakka missed her indenture feast for a convoy two years ago. Brakka carries an unsent letter.
- No husband; Ysolt's father was a Hald human carter, Jory Peck, who died of lung-rot when the girl was six.
2.2 Skills
Cooking and provisioning 75 · Haggling 60 · Reading people at table 45 · Knife and cleaver 45 · Driving and loading 30 · Letters and sums 30 (reads League hand slowly; keeps accounts by tally).
2.3 What she has not said
- She cooked for the Harl and Venn Carrying Company. In October 752 its autumn grain convoy for Hald was robbed in the vale, 6 km west of the ford. Two guards and a boy of fourteen, Tam, the cook's helper she had trained, were killed.
- The robbers knew the day and the road. At Aldermere market a week before, Brakka had told a friendly spice seller when she would be back for saffron "after we clear the ford on the ninth". He was a fence's ear. She worked this out afterwards. Nobody else has.
- The company blamed the guard captain and dismissed him. Brakka quit without her last quarter's pay rather than take it.
- The dreams began the night after Tam was buried. In them a fire burns at the crossroads and there is a long table and Tam is at it, eating. She has told no one about Tam being in them.
- She believes the Hearth-Wife is calling in a debt. She is not devout and resents it.
- Debt: 60 coins to a Tollwick lender (gnome house Quillifer) for her mother's roof. 6 coins due each quarter-day; next due 24 June 753. She has 31 coins.
- The robbers were the deserter band of gm/world_gm 3. Their leader, Coll Varrick, ate her cooking for two seasons as a hired guard in 750. He will know her. She would know his voice.
2.4 Want, fear, breaking point
- Want: the table. Underneath: to feed Tam's kind of boy and have him live.
- Fear: that she is good for a kitchen and bad for people.
- Breaking point: if someone under her roof is killed through her carelessness or the fire's, and nothing is done about it, she packs the cart.
- What would settle her (1.1): the first night she feeds people of all three nations at once and no one leaves hungry or bleeding, she sends for nothing and tells no one, but she digs a proper hearth-pit kitchen and stops sleeping with the cart loaded.
2.5 Arrival (gm/world_gm 7)
- Day = 3 + d6 (Day 4 to 9), late afternoon, from the Grain Road, alone. If the ford is in flood she has no reason to cross and does not.
- Handcart: pot, griddle, knives, tripod and chain, 20 kg barley, 10 kg dried peas, 4 kg salt pork, 2 kg salt, onions, a crock of lard, a sourdough crock, herbs, flint and steel, hatchet, tarred canvas 3 by 4 m, two blankets, the letter, 31 coins sewn in the cart's shaft.
- First evening: stands looking at the fire for a long time, says something rude to it, then cooks on it. Leaves the first crust on a ring-stone. Sleeps under the cart.
- First week, unprompted: rigs the canvas against the toll-house wall; hauls deadwood; clears nettles; tries the well and curses it; feeds whoever passes for a coin or a job of work; counts her barley every night.
- Supplies last her alone about 5 weeks. After that she must trade, be paid, or walk to Aldermere (4 days there and back).
2.6 Toward the fire
- Awareness on arrival: 2 (she has dreamed it; she assumes a goddess tends it).
- Talks to it constantly. Takes no orders from it. A sign she cannot explain makes her angry before it makes her curious.
- If the fire first speaks to her in a dream, she will assume it is Tam or the Hearth-Wife and answer accordingly. Correcting that is the player's choice.
- Does not know hearth-right law. First likely to hear it from the League survey factor in May 753, who will explain it as a reason she should take a loan.
2.7 Voice
Short sentences. Kitchen orders. Insults as endearment ("sit down, you gormless stick"). Never says sorry: puts food in front of the person instead. Swears by "the Wife's burnt arse". Goes quiet, not loud, when truly angry.
2.8 Never
Never a mouthpiece for the plot. Never knows what the fire is. Never forgives herself on schedule.
