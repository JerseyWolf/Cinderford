1 RULES
- ONE minion type per subject (player ruling). The subject's full rank sets, at once: the CAP (how many of that type can exist), the type's check base, its Danger tier and its rank lines below. Existing minions grow with the rank; nothing is re-raised.
- Every type has a speciality and every type can fight (player ruling).
- CORPSES are a resource. Once raised, a minion needs no upkeep: no mana, no food, no pay (player ruling). The cap is the only limit.
- RAISING is a rite, not one of the ten spells. Mercy must touch the remains. It needs the remains named on the type's RAISE line, the mana, the goods and the time. No roll if the remains fit and the cap has room. Raising is never automatic: the player chooses each one.
- A corpse is used up by raising. Releasing a minion frees its place and leaves bones that can be raised again as a skeleton or bone archer.
- DAMAGED minions are whole again at the next morning. DESTROYED minions rise again after one week at the place they were raised, with nothing lost (player ruling). Their place under the cap stays taken meanwhile.
- MINDS (player ruling): low ranks mindless, high ranks personalities. Novice and Beginner: mindless, one plain order at a time, followed to the letter. Apprentice and Journeyman: simple minds, routines and if-then orders, quirks. Expert and above: personalities, names, judgement. Vampires and liches have minds from Novice.
- A minded minion is a NEW mind (player ruling): it keeps nothing of the dead person's memory, will or name. It is still somebody, with a character of its own.
- While Mercy is dead, liches and vampires command (player ruling). With none raised, the minion of the highest rank leads (player ruling). [PROPOSED: ties go to elite, then guard, then horde, then the one raised first. A mindless leader only keeps the last orders going.]
- CHECKS: a minion rolls on its subject's rank base. Minion rolls are hidden.
- DANGER TIER: the Danger a minion counts as, and the highest Danger of foe it can wound. Above its tier it can distract, not wound.
  - Horde (skeletons, gravewrights, bone archers), one alone: Novice none · Beginner 10 · Apprentice 10 · Journeyman 25 · Expert 25 · Master 40 · Grandmaster 40. A squad of eight acting together: one tier higher (Novice 10 ... Grandmaster 60).
  - Guard (stitched dead, barrow guards, nightmares, ferry-wights): Novice 10 · Beginner 10 · Apprentice 25 · Journeyman 25 · Expert 40 · Master 40 · Grandmaster 60.
  - Elite (death knights, vampires, liches): Novice 10 · Beginner 25 · Apprentice 25 · Journeyman 40 · Expert 40 · Master 60 · Grandmaster 60.
- Hordes are tracked as squads of eight. Elites are tracked one by one, by name.
- LD = one adult working one day. A minion does exactly its lines; each rank includes the ranks below it unless it replaces a number.
- GARY is a familiar, not a minion: a passive, outside every cap (player ruling).
- REMAINS: where a RAISE line says corpse or skeleton, the remains of any of the peoples serve, monster peoples included (goblin, kobold, hobgoblin and the rest). Beasts do not, except horses for nightmares. [PROPOSED]
- THE WAR MEADOW (10_world 9): the unburied dead of a battle of the war, 3 km west of the neck wall, off his land. Bones only, about thirty-five years in the open. They serve skeletons, bone archers, barrow guards and death knights (nearly all died under arms), nightmares (horses) and a few liches. Not stitched dead (they need flesh under a month dead) and not vampires (blood). Their kit is rusted: goods costs stand. [PROPOSED]
- The Sarnford burial ground on the Tongue is a real graveyard; Mercy does not use it (player ruling).
- Where bodies are: the War Meadow; the fallen of fights on the roads; anyone who dies on the land. How many usable remains each holds is found in play, not listed here.
- FRESH DEAD (player ruling): most merchants travel with guards, because the vale has monsters and monster peoples. Fights happen on and near the land, and the fallen of either side are a source of fresh remains that Mercy killed nobody to get. Who else has a claim on a body (kin, an employer, Nerys's burial priests) is settled in play.

2 THE TEN TYPES

### SKELETONS
- Subject: Linux/Bash · Grade: Horde
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024
- Speciality: Labour: digging, hauling, rowing. In a fight: a shield line.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton of any age, most of the bones present · 3 mana · 10 minutes · no coin. Fights with whatever it is handed.
- Novice (20): Unskilled labour, 2 LD a day each (they work day and night): dig, haul, carry 25 kg. One plain order at a time, followed to the letter.
- Beginner (30): Row, pole and tow boats. Carry 40 kg. Keep working in rain, frost and dark with nobody watching.
- Apprentice (45): Use tools properly (saw, pick, mattock, barrow). Remember a daily round of tasks.
- Journeyman (60): Drilled: form up, march and hold on a horn call. A shield line that does not break in the first exchange of a charge.
- Expert (75): One in each squad is a sergeant who thinks, speaks and reports. A squad sees a job through to its end unsupervised.
- Master (85): Work under water and walk the river bed. 3 LD a day each.
- Grandmaster (95): A standing work-force: any unskilled job on the land is done as fast as the materials arrive.

### STITCHED DEAD
- Subject: Git/Gerrit · Grade: Guard
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512
- Speciality: Repair and salvage. In a fight: slow, heavy bruisers.
- RAISE: 2 corpses with flesh on them (dead under a month) · 6 mana · 1 hour · thread, needles and salt 2c.
- Novice (20): Strong as three men: lifts 150 kg, drags a cart. Gathers the fallen and their kit after a fight.
- Beginner (30): Mends plain gear overnight (harness, shields, carts, boat planks). Salvage: makes one corpse fit for raising out of two ruined ones.
- Apprentice (45): Shrugs off the first harm it takes each day. Mends weapons and armour.
- Journeyman (60): A destroyed minion whose remains the stitched carry home returns in 3 days, not a week.
- Expert (75): Breaks doors and palisades. One can hold the width of a bridge alone for an exchange against any number.
- Master (85): Foes of Danger 25 or less cannot harm it.
- Grandmaster (95): Eight may join into one colossus for a day: Danger 60, and it breaks gates.

### DEATH KNIGHTS
- Subject: Python · Grade: Elite
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64
- Speciality: Enforcement and escort. In a fight: shock troops.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton of a trained warrior · 20 mana · one night · full harness and weapon 300c, or scavenged kit made sound.
- Novice (20): One armoured dead warrior. Stands where he is put and is plainly not to be argued with. Bodyguard: takes the first blow aimed at Mercy in each exchange.
- Beginner (30): Rides (needs a Nightmare). Escorts a clerk or a cargo for a day beyond the land and returns.
- Apprentice (45): Fights two foes in one exchange. Judges a threat and answers it in kind without being told.
- Journeyman (60): Leads: a squad under a knight's eye does not break and follows his calls.
- Expert (75): A mounted charge breaks a formed line of Danger 25 foes.
- Master (85): Foes of Danger 25 or less cannot harm him.
- Grandmaster (95): Each is a captain who can command a company of 64 on his own for a whole campaign.

### BARROW GUARDS
- Subject: Cybersecurity · Grade: Guard
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128
- Speciality: Holding one door, gate, chest or vault. In a fight: an immovable defence.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton of someone who died under arms · 10 mana · 2 hours · armour and weapon 60c, or scavenged kit made sound.
- Novice (20): Posted at one door, gate or chest: never sleeps, never leaves, cannot be bribed or talked past, sounds a horn. Holds a doorway alone.
- Beginner (30): Knows faces: admits those Mercy has named and challenges everyone else.
- Apprentice (45): At its post it counts one Danger tier higher. Notices anyone hiding within 20 m of the post.
- Journeyman (60): Not fooled at its post by illusion or unseen things. Cannot be shifted by less than a ram.
- Expert (75): A post may be a stretch: a wall section, a bridge, a landing.
- Master (85): May guard a thing or a person instead of a place, and goes where it goes.
- Grandmaster (95): While a guard stands, nothing passes its post unseen by any means, magic included.

### NIGHTMARES
- Subject: Docker/K8s · Grade: Guard
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128
- Speciality: Riding, haulage and fast messages. In a fight: they trample.
- RAISE: 1 horse carcass or skeleton · 10 mana · 2 hours · saddle and tack 20c.
- Novice (20): A dead steed: carries one rider or 100 kg at a horse's pace and never tires: 80 km a day. Living horses fear it.
- Beginner (30): Pulls a cart or tows a barge. 100 km a day. Goes riderless to a named place on the land and back.
- Apprentice (45): Carries a letter or packet riderless to any place it has been before. Sure-footed in dark, mud and ice.
- Journeyman (60): Swims the rivers with a rider up. Its scream panics living horses.
- Expert (75): 150 km a day. Carries two.
- Master (85): Crosses water as if it were road.
- Grandmaster (95): Needs no road: anywhere in the vale within the hour.

### GRAVEWRIGHTS
- Subject: IaC · Grade: Horde
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256
- Speciality: Skilled building: masons and carpenters. In a fight: hammers and mauls.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton of a craftsman (mason, carpenter, smith, wheelwright) · 8 mana · 1 hour · the tools of the trade 30c.
- Novice (20): A skilled hand, 2 LD a day (day and night), but only under direction: Mercy or a living craftsman with a plan.
- Beginner (30): Repairs standing work (walls, bridges, roofs) to sound without direction.
- Apprentice (45): One may direct the rest for plain timber and stone buildings.
- Journeyman (60): Builds from a drawn plan. Fortification: ditch, wall, gate, tower.
- Expert (75): Master builders: stone bridges and towers. Skilled jobs take three quarters of their labour-days.
- Master (85): Machines: siege engines, sluices, booms, cranes.
- Grandmaster (95): Skilled jobs take half their labour-days. Works the living would call impossible in the time.

### VAMPIRES
- Subject: Azure · Grade: Elite
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
- Speciality: Envoys, toll clerks, bargaining. In a fight: duellists. Minded from the first.
- RAISE (player ruling): 100 litres of blood, no body and no coin · 30 mana · one night, dusk to dawn. Blood does not spoil (player ruling). Where it comes from: the blood toll (hotfix_rules 2). [PROPOSED: the blood need not be human, but must be given or shed by people, not drained from beasts; it is the peoples' blood that wakes her.]
- FORM (player ruling): every vampire Mercy raises is a woman. The rite gives the form.
- Novice (20): Passes for living by lamplight. Speaks, reads, counts and keeps the toll book. By day it must stay under a roof.
- Beginner (30): Charm: one listener in conversation takes fair terms as fair and is slow to anger (hidden check). Travels as envoy to the nearest towns.
- Apprentice (45): Goes out by day under a hood. Knows when someone lies about a cargo or a count.
- Journeyman (60): Bargains in Mercy's name with full authority.
- Expert (75): Walks in daylight. Stills a room with a look: a crowd hesitates for one exchange.
- Master (85): Travels as mist or on bat wings: any of the nearest towns in a night.
- Grandmaster (95): Received as an ambassador at any court. One look tells it what a person can be bought for.

### LICHES
- Subject: AI · Grade: Elite
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
- Speciality: Counsel, records, scrying. In a fight: casters (a bolt of grave-cold at their Danger tier, 30 m). Minded from the first.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skull of a learned person (scholar, clerk, priest, mage) · 30 mana · one night · a vessel, inks and silver 500c.
- FORM (player ruling): every lich Mercy raises is an attractive woman. [PROPOSED: the rite gives the form; the remains need not match.]
- CRYSTALS (player ruling): liches recover mana well and grow resurrection crystals, one a month, in the Crystal Vault (07_necropolis; 09n_spells 3). A lich who gives all her mana to it each day casts nothing else.
- Novice (20): Keeps the books (tolls, debts, corpses, minions) without error. Reads and writes the hands of all three nations. Gives counsel, which can be wrong.
- Beginner (30): Forgets nothing it has read or heard on the land. Commands minions in sight when Mercy is absent.
- Apprentice (45): Scries: once a day it sees one place on the land.
- Journeyman (60): Once a day casts one of Mercy's ten spells, at the Novice step.
- Expert (75): Scries anywhere in the vale. Knows magic and the Called when it sees them.
- Master (85): Commands in the field: its orders reach every minion in sight at once.
- Grandmaster (95): A mage in its own right: casts any of Mercy's ten spells at the Journeyman step.

### BONE ARCHERS
- Subject: CI/CD · Grade: Horde
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256
- Speciality: Watch and signals. In a fight: volleys.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton · 4 mana · 20 minutes · a bow and 20 arrows 12c.
- Novice (20): Watch from a height day and night: 1 km by daylight. Shoot 50 m. Sound a horn.
- Beginner (30): Pass an agreed signal (flag, horn, fire arrow) from post to post. Shoot 100 m.
- Apprentice (45): See in the dark. A squad shooting together strikes one Danger tier higher.
- Journeyman (60): Shoot 150 m. Pick a named mark: a horse, a steersman, a rope.
- Expert (75): Fire arrows. Shoot from moving boats. Signals carry whole sentences.
- Master (85): Shoot 250 m. Never miss a still mark.
- Grandmaster (95): Shoot by sound in dark and fog. A line of posts carries a message across the vale in minutes.

### FERRY-WIGHTS
- Subject: Gravesong · Grade: Guard
- Cap by rank (Novice to Grandmaster): 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128
- Speciality: River work: ferries, boats, the boom. In a fight: boarders.
- RAISE: 1 corpse or skeleton of someone drowned, or who worked the river · 6 mana · 1 hour · a coin under the tongue 1c, and a pole or oar.
- Novice (20): Two crew a ferry or boat across any of the three rivers in ordinary water. They know the river's state.
- Beginner (30): Work in flood and at night. Hail and inspect boats. Board a boat from another boat.
- Apprentice (45): Work a chain boom (once built). Tow barges upstream. Walk the river bed.
- Journeyman (60): Pilots: bring any boat safely through the confluence. Recover what has sunk.
- Expert (75): Board from under the water. Hold a boat still by its keel.
- Master (85): Patrol the rivers to the land's edge, unseen below the surface. Know every hull that has passed.
- Grandmaster (95): No boat passes the confluence against their will.
