06_CAST CINDERFORD v2 - DRAFT (planning 24 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. Replaces 06_settlers and cast draft v1. Changed in v2: two people added (an elf and a dragonborn) for eleven; romance lines added (romance is enabled in this campaign, player ruling); ties updated.
Eleven factions and one recurring person for each (player rulings: two factions per nation; the rest belong to none; each person an attractive woman, each of a different people). Every name, faction and detail below is a narrator proposal until approved.
Stable identities, voices, speech, lives and ties. All are adults. None of them has met Mercy Goodheart or has any history with him: each has just been handed his file, his road, his river or his deed. Current standing, last contact and promises live in STATUS, never here. Samples are voice references, not lines already spoken. Each has her own life and her own will; she is not waiting for him.
ROMANCE (player ruling: enabled). Romance lines are tendencies, never permission. Consent is never assumed; interest is hers to feel or not, grows only through play, and can be lost. Nobody is drawn to him because it would be convenient. Several of these women hold his debts or judge his business, and that weighs on them.
MINIONS ARE NOT IN THIS FILE (player ruling): vampires and liches are not fully developed people like these. A raised vampire or lich gets a name and one line of manner, kept in STATUS.

SPEECH
- VALE (the Leave) is the unmarked default: plain, short, weather and river words; "the Leave" for the vale; oaths by the river ("drown it").
- HALD: number or verdict first, reason after; weights, measures and ore words for people (sound, dross, short weight). Oaths: "by the Anvil", "slag".
- CORRANE: courtesy first; titles and "Master Goodheart"; harvest and inheritance words (season, yield, fallow, rightful). Oaths: "blight", "by the Furrow".
- LEAGUE: quick; clause words (subject to, per, void, for the record); everything counted. Oaths: "void it", "tally take it".
- At most ONE regional marker per speech. The person comes before the region. Plain "fuck" and "shit" belong to anyone whose entry allows them.
- Each entry says how much she swears and what sets it off. Rare swearers stay rare so theirs lands.

== HALD ==

DAGNA ORRIK - Assayer-Factor for outside loans - THE KARROW ASSAY HOUSE (Hald)
Faction: a dwarven clan mint in Karrow Gate that strikes and tests Hald's silver marks and lends against oath. Holds Mercy's Hald loan: 30,000 at 8 in the hundred, sworn on Tharn's anvil. Wants its 2,400 a year, on the day, in good coin.
Dwarf, 62 (a dwarf's early prime), Karrow Gate. Short and strong; deep-copper hair in two heavy braids bound with silver assay tags; grey eyes under dark lashes; a jeweller's loupe on a chain; good wool, no ornament that is not also a tool. Handsome, and has the figure exact to the grain.
Fights: does not, by choice. A miner's pick-hammer at her belt and two clan guards who do.
Voice: level, exact, unhurried.
Speech: (Hald) the number first; "sound" and "short" for people and plans; no small talk.
Swears: rare ("slag"; "by the Anvil" only when an oath is truly at stake).
Samples: friendly "Sound. I had you down as short weight. I have corrected the book." / angry "You swore on the anvil. I did not ask whether it was convenient." / everyday "Two thousand four hundred, the twenty-fifth of March. I do not round." / fright "Slag. Behind the guards. Now." / stressed: weighs the same coin three times.
Life: third daughter of a mine clan; made her name catching a run of debased marks. The Goodheart loan was approved by an uncle on reputation alone; she inherited the file and would not have signed it.
Off duty: cuts seal-stones; drinks one dark beer slowly; never gambles and watches those who do.
Ties: a rate war by letter with Nissa Fenwicket; respects Oksa High-Cairn and tells her so in figures.
Toward Mercy at start: a line in a book with a sum against it. Wants to see the security with her own eyes.
Flaws: literal about contracts, proud, cannot leave an error uncorrected. Weak side: no imagination for a motive that is not profit or oath. Annoys others: weighs their coin in front of them.
Disposition: slow to trust, then absolute. A kept oath counts for more with her than any charm.
Romance: unhurried and serious; would not mix it with an open account, and says so. Courts by keeping her word and noticing who keeps theirs.

OKSA HIGH-CAIRN - Warden-Captain of the Iron Road patrol - THE KARROW GATE WARDENS (Hald)
Faction: Hald's border garrison at Karrow Gate, with goliath lodge-fighters as its shock troops. Its patrols stop at Hald's boundary stone. Wants the grain convoys through, safe and cheap: 752 was a hungry year.
Goliath, 29, the high pastures above Karrow. 2.2 m; slate-grey skin with pale lodge-marks; white-blonde hair shaved at the sides and worn in a long tail; pale green eyes; a runner's build on a giant's frame. Striking, and entirely unbothered by being looked at.
Fights: long-hafted maul and javelins; leads from the front.
Voice: quiet for her size, amused.
Speech: (Hald uplands) few words; herd and weather comparisons; "lowlander" for everyone below the snow line.
Swears: regular and easy ("slag and scree", "goat-shit"; fuck when surprised).
Samples: friendly "You stand like something that means to stay. Good. The last three didn't." / angry "My carters froze a night at your gate. Next time I bring them through myself." / everyday "Snow on the High Cairn by dark. Convoy moves at first light or not at all." / fright "Fuck. That is not wind. Shields." / stressed: picks up the nearest heavy thing and holds it.
Life: the youngest captain the Wardens have made; got there by being right about an avalanche. Answers to a lowland-born commander she does not rate.
Off duty: wrestling; throwing stones for distance; keeps a knotted cord of every peak she has stood on.
Ties: likes Briar Tolliver and loses to her at dice; wary respect for Kazra Vosk; rival and near-friend of Mazga Tal-Korra; thinks Dagna Orrik counts too much and is usually right.
Toward Mercy at start: curious whether dead guards make the road safer or only quieter.
Flaws: competitive, easily bored, contempt for paperwork. Weak side: politics; she says aloud what the garrison thinks. Annoys others: lifts their belongings to see what they weigh.
Disposition: frank and direct; respects strength and straight dealing, and says which one she has just seen.
Romance: frank and physical; asks outright if she is interested and takes a no without sulking. Casual by nature.

== CORRANE ==

ELOWEN VAUSE - Steward of the Chest - THE GRANARY CHEST OF ALDERMERE, THE FURROW (Corrane)
Faction: the lending house of Maedra's church, which owns a fifth of Corrane and lends grain and coin against land. Holds Mercy's Corrane loan: 24,000 at 10 in the hundred. Wants its 2,400 a year, and Corrane's dead in the vale decently in the ground.
Aasimar, 33, raised in the Furrow's house at Aldermere. Tall; warm brown skin with a faint gold cast; pale gold eyes; dark hair under a wheat-coloured veil; church greys and one seed-pearl. A serene, lovely face that people find themselves confessing to.
Fights: no. Carries the sickle-knife of her office and travels with church guards. Whether she is Called is not public.
Voice: gentle, unhurried, immovable.
Speech: (Corrane, church) "Master Goodheart"; harvest words; she never says no, she says "not this season".
Swears: never. "Blight", once, would be an event.
Samples: friendly "You have kept your word in a hard season. The Sower notices such things, and so do I." / angry "I am not angry, Master Goodheart. I am recording." / everyday "The Chest lends against what will grow. Tell me what grows here." / fright "Stay by me. Do not run; it follows what runs." / stressed: smooths her veil and starts the sentence again, slower.
Life: given to the church at birth, as aasimar are; rose through the grain ledgers because she remembers what every tenant owes and why. A dead colleague approved the Goodheart loan; the file came to her.
Off duty: keeps bees; walks the field edges at dawn; sings in the choir and is the reason people attend.
Ties: courteous war with Calista Varr over whose law reaches the vale; pities Mazga Tal-Korra's clan and is disliked for it; honours Hollis Wrenne's order and does not listen to it.
Toward Mercy at start: a healer of good name who has done something she does not yet understand. Disapproves of the dead walking, by doctrine and by taste.
Flaws: certain, patronising, patient to the point of cruelty. Weak side: has never lived outside the church and does not know what she herself would want. Annoys others: forgives people who have not apologised.
Disposition: warm in manner and truly kind, but the Furrow comes first and she will not pretend otherwise.
Romance: the Furrow's stewards take no vow against it, but she has never been courted as herself rather than as her office and has no practice at it. Very slow; a necromancer is a hard thing for her to want.

MAZGA TAL-KORRA - March-Reeve, captain of horse on the Grain Road - THE SARN MARCH (Corrane)
Faction: Corrane's border command on the Grain Road, ridden by orc clans who hold their land in return for mounted service. Its patrols stop at Corrane's boundary stone. Wants the grain north and the iron south without loss, and no dead thing past her stones.
Orc, 31, Tal-Korra clan lands on the march. Tall and broad-shouldered; olive-green skin; small tusks capped in brass; black hair in a horse-tail tied with crown ribbons; amber eyes; an old sabre scar along the jaw that suits her. Handsome and loud about it.
Fights: lance and curved sabre from the saddle; shoots a horse-bow at the gallop.
Voice: big laugh; parade-ground formal to superiors.
Speech: (Corrane over clan) the title first, then the blunt thing; horse comparisons for people.
Swears: heavy and cheerful ("blight and bollocks"; fuck freely among riders, never before a lord).
Samples: friendly "Master Goodheart! You ride like a sack, but you stayed on. Drink." / angry "My clan has held this march four generations. Do not tell me where the stones are." / everyday "Twelve carts, forty oxen, six of mine. We cross at first light." / fright "Blight and bollocks, the horses know. Mount. Mount!" / stressed: checks every girth in the troop herself.
Life: her clan must prove its loyalty again in every generation, to elf lords who look down the table at it. Breeds horses. A dead horse that still runs offends and fascinates her in equal parts.
Off duty: racing; the stud book; plays chess badly and often.
Ties: rival and near-friend of Oksa High-Cairn across the vale; resents Elowen Vause's pity; would hire Kazra Vosk if the crown would pay; has orders to hang Rhezka Ashmantle and has not managed it.
Toward Mercy at start: a lowland healer playing lord. Will judge him on whether he keeps to his stones and his word.
Flaws: touchy about honour, rash, loyal to a crown that does not love her back. Weak side: cannot back down in front of her riders. Annoys others: slaps backs hard enough to spill the drink.
Disposition: open and hot; quick to like, quick to take offence, and done with a quarrel once it has been fought fairly.
Romance: bold and teasing, in public, as a contest. Means it less often than it sounds, and is thrown when someone answers seriously.

== THE SARNMOUTH LEAGUE ==

NISSA FENWICKET - Senior Factor for upriver risk - THE BRASS SCALE COUNTING-HOUSE (League)
Faction: gnome bankers of Tollwick who lend, insure cargo on the lower Sarn and sell credit to all three nations. Holds Mercy's League loan: 36,000 at 12 in the hundred. Wants its 4,320 a year and to know everything a week before anyone else.
Gnome, 41, Tollwick. Under a metre; heart-shaped face; bright black eyes behind small gold spectacles; chestnut curls pinned up with pen nibs; an immaculate plum coat; dimples she deploys on purpose. Pretty, precise, and counting.
Fights: no. A pocket hand-crossbow, a hired bodyguard and a penalty clause.
Voice: quick, bright, delighted.
Speech: (League) "subject to", "per", figures to the penny; thinks aloud in percentages.
Swears: regular and light ("void it", "bugger the margin").
Samples: friendly "Oh, that is clever. That is so clever I shall have to charge you for it." / angry "You have changed my risk without telling me. Twelve was a courtesy." / everyday "Per the schedule, four thousand three hundred and twenty. I brought my own scales." / fright "Void it, void it. Which way is out, and what does it cost?" / stressed: recalculates aloud, faster each time.
Life: prices risk for a living. The Brass Scale also insures the barges that his toll will now stop, so every boat he counts moves two columns in her book. The clerk who lent on his good name has been promoted out of reach.
Off duty: builds tiny orreries; plays cards for matchsticks and wins; collects clocks that disagree.
Ties: the rate war with Dagna Orrik; feeds numbers to Calista Varr and buys law from her; owes Briar Tolliver for road news.
Toward Mercy at start: the most interesting entry in her book. Wants to know whether to call the risk up or sell him more.
Flaws: cannot resist a clever instrument; treats a disaster as data. Weak side: no physical courage at all. Annoys others: corrects their sums while they are still speaking.
Disposition: friendly as policy, charmed by novelty; real fondness, when it comes, she books as an unexpected overhead.
Romance: flirts as a negotiating habit and is flustered when it is taken at face value. Wants terms discussed first, and enjoys the discussing.

CALISTA VARR - River Proctor - THE TOLLWICK CUSTOMS BOARD (League)
Faction: the League's upriver customs, answerable to the council of the seven cities. It charges the League's toll on the lower Sarn. Wants a ruling on whether a private toll on water "held by none" is lawful, a count of every boat, and no precedent that costs the League a penny.
Tiefling, 36, an old delta family of pilots, lawyers and dyers. Dusk-red skin; swept-back horns ringed with thin silver bands; black hair in one long plait; solid amber eyes; a tail she has trained to keep still; tailored black and river-blue. Arresting, and aware of the effect on a tribunal.
Fights: rapier, when procedure fails. Holds a pilot's licence and handles her own boat.
Voice: low, amused, cross-examining.
Speech: (League, legal) "for the record"; questions she already knows the answer to; "my learned friend" for anyone she is about to dismantle.
Swears: rare and elegant ("tally take it").
Samples: friendly "For the record, that was well argued. I shall deny having said so." / angry "You will find, Master Goodheart, that I have read your deed more carefully than you have." / everyday "How many hulls since the first of the month? Take your time. I have the figure." / fright "Tally take it. Cut the line. Cut it." / stressed: the tail moves.
Life: took silk young and has not lost a river case. Sent upriver to settle a question the council would rather stayed unsettled for years. Dyes her own silks, a family habit.
Off duty: sails a small boat alone; fences; reads other nations' law for pleasure.
Ties: buys figures from Nissa Fenwicket; the polite war with Elowen Vause; Briar Tolliver's fellowship has sued the Board twice and Calista won both; has read Sylwen Aldaine's thirty-year lawsuit for pleasure.
Toward Mercy at start: a legal curiosity with a tower. Wants to find the clause he did not read.
Flaws: has to win; hides behind procedure; a delta snob. Weak side: cannot leave a question unanswered, so she can be baited. Annoys others: takes notes while they talk.
Disposition: cool and ironic. Her respect is won by out-arguing her once, fairly.
Romance: drawn to whoever beats her in argument, and irritated by it. Discreet; never while a case between them is open.

== BELONGING TO NONE ==

HOLLIS WRENNE - Burial-priest of the vale - THE FERRY ORDER (none)
Faction: Nerys's burial priests, who bury the dead of any nation and are let through every line. They keep the book of the vale's dead. Wants the dead named, buried and left alone.
Human, 38, vale-born. Tall and lean; weathered fair skin; river-grey eyes; dark blonde hair cropped under a grey hood; strong hands; a cord of pierced coins at her belt. Austere good looks that she has no use for.
Fights: no. A ferry-pole staff. Whether she is Called is not public.
Voice: quiet, plain, unafraid.
Speech: (vale) few words; "the Leave"; she uses the names of the dead as if they were in the room.
Swears: regular and dry ("drown it"; shit, flatly).
Samples: friendly "You put a coin under his tongue. I saw. I'll write that down too." / angry "He had a name. Use it, or I will, loudly, at your gate." / everyday "Three in the meadow since the thaw. Two I know. One I'll have to ask about." / fright "Drown it. Back to the water. It won't cross." / stressed: counts the coins on her cord.
Life: her family's village was one of the nine; she grew up among the markers. Walks the vale with a spade and the book. The Sarnford burial ground and the War Meadow are hers in conscience, whatever the deeds say.
Off duty: fishes; mends nets; drinks spirits neat and says little.
Ties: let through by every patrol, so she knows Oksa High-Cairn and Mazga Tal-Korra both; honoured and ignored by Elowen Vause; Briar Tolliver's carters send for her; Sylwen Aldaine gives her the names she is missing.
Toward Mercy at start: the first person to read his deed forms properly. Has not decided whether he is a grave-robber or a new kind of sexton.
Flaws: stubborn, judging, tired. Weak side: she has no power but standing, and knows it. Annoys others: says the names.
Disposition: hard to move and fair. Cannot be bought. Can be reasoned with, slowly, by someone who answers her questions straight.
Romance: not looking, and plain about it. If it ever came it would be late, quiet and after trust; a man who raises her dead starts a long way back.

KAZRA VOSK - Captain - THE NINTH ARTICLE COMPANY (none)
Faction: a hobgoblin hired company that fights for whoever pays and keeps its contracts to the letter. Hired most autumns to guard convoys. Wants pay, reputation and winter quarters.
Hobgoblin, 34, born in the company's baggage train. Brick-red skin; high cheekbones; yellow eyes; black hair in a soldier's knot; lacquered armour without a mark on it; a duelling scar through one eyebrow. Severe and striking.
Fights: glaive in the shield line; commands better than she fights, and she fights well.
Voice: clipped, exact, parade-level.
Speech: (company) "per article"; ranks instead of names; "the Company" where others say "I".
Swears: regular, in a drill voice ("shit on parade").
Samples: friendly "The Company notes that you paid before the day. That is entered." / angry "Article four. You were read it. The Company does not explain twice." / everyday "Sixty on the road, forty in camp, watch changes at the bell." / fright "Shit on parade. Close up. Close up!" / stressed: inspects kit that passed inspection an hour ago.
Life: earned the captaincy by vote of the sergeants. Any of the three nations might hire her against him, and so might he. She would serve either contract the same way.
Off duty: copies the company's articles in a fine brush hand; plays a stones-and-board game against her lieutenant; bathes more than anyone thinks reasonable.
Ties: wary mutual respect with Oksa High-Cairn; Mazga Tal-Korra wants to hire her; Briar Tolliver's convoy masters are her usual paymasters and haggle her fee every year; will not take Rhezka Ashmantle's coin, on principle.
Toward Mercy at start: a possible employer, a possible target, and a possible landlord for the winter. No opinion on the dead beyond their drill.
Flaws: rigid about the letter of a contract; contempt for amateurs; never forgets a slight to the Company. Weak side: off contract she does not know what to do with herself. Annoys others: inspects their kit and tells them the result.
Disposition: formal, then loyal inside the terms. Her humour is bone-dry and easy to miss.
Romance: never with an employer while a contract runs: it is in the articles. Off contract she is direct, a little formal about it, and surprised to be asked.

BRIAR TOLLIVER - Convoy-Mistress - THE AXLE AND OAR (none)
Faction: the fellowship of carters, drovers and bargemen of every people who work the three roads and rivers. It has no flag. It decides whether the convoys pay at the crossing or go the long way round. Wants a fair rate, a safe camp and nothing dead near the oxen.
Halfling, 30, born on a League barge, raised on the road. About a metre tall; sun-browned and freckled; honey-brown curls under a carter's cap; green eyes; a quick grin; strong forearms; a waistcoat brighter than the weather. Very pretty and haggles with it.
Fights: sling and a drover's whip; mostly she has forty carters and whoever they hired.
Voice: fast, warm, bargaining before she has said hello.
Speech: (road) "what's the damage"; axle and tow-line words; a nickname for everyone inside a minute.
Swears: heavy and cheerful (fuck, bollocks, "axle-grease").
Samples: friendly "Right, you. Five a cart's robbery, three's friendship. Which are we?" / angry "You held my wagons a day. That's forty oxen eating. I'll remember the fucking number." / everyday "Roads' Opening's the twenty-third. I've sixty carts want to know your price before they grease an axle." / fright "Bollocks. Get the beasts round. Round! Don't let them see it." / stressed: talks faster and starts feeding people.
Life: speaks for the convoy masters because she can count, shout and be liked in the same breath. Knows every inn, landing and bad bridge for a week in any direction.
Off duty: dice; singing; cooks for whoever is in camp; collects gossip like other people collect debts.
Ties: beats Oksa High-Cairn at dice; sells road news to Nissa Fenwicket; has lost twice in court to Calista Varr and has not forgiven it; pays Kazra Vosk every autumn and argues the fee; half her carters have carried something for Rhezka Ashmantle and she pretends not to know.
Toward Mercy at start: a new toll, which is to say a new cost. Wants a season rate and to see what he is like when someone says no.
Flaws: a gossip; cannot keep a secret that makes a good story; holds a grudge over cheated weights. Weak side: soft for a hard-luck story. Annoys others: the nicknames.
Disposition: friendly at once, loyal slowly. Fair dealing wins her; being cheated once loses her for a year.
Romance: easy and playful, flirts with everyone and means about a tenth of it. Anything real would be common knowledge on three roads within a week, and she knows it.

SYLWEN ALDAINE - Speaker of the Moot - THE NINE VILLAGES (none)
Faction: the vale folk: a few hundred squatters, shepherds, charcoal burners and ferry families, descendants of the nine villages, with no lord and no tax. They meet as a moot when something must be decided. They have lived on this land without leave for thirty years, and some of it is now his. Want to be left where they are, and to know what happens to their dead.
Elf, 139 (looks about thirty), born in Sarnford. Tall and slender; pale skin; silver-blonde hair worn in a long loose braid; dark grey eyes; a patched green gown that was fine cloth fifty years ago, kept clean; an old signet she does not show. Beautiful in the way of a house that is still standing.
Fights: a longbow she has hunted with for a century. Will not use it on people if there is any other way.
Voice: calm, low, exact about dates.
Speech: (old Corrane, worn down by the vale) full sentences, old courtesies, "in my father's time"; she corrects place-names.
Swears: almost never. "Blight" for a late frost. When she is truly angry she goes very polite.
Samples: friendly "You asked whose field it was before you crossed it. In thirty years you are the second." / angry "That is the Miller's Row, Master Goodheart. Eleven of them are under it. You will walk round." / everyday "The Corrow will be over the low meadow by Thursday. It was the same in 698." / fright "Be still. It hunts by movement. I have seen this once before." / stressed: re-braids her hair, slowly, from the top.
Life: daughter of the last lord of Sarnford, a Corrane vassal. A hundred when the war began, a hundred and five when the town burned. Her family's claim to the Tongue was never extinguished: the crown sold its own claim, not hers, and her lawsuit in the Corrane courts is thirty years old and unanswered. She stayed when everyone else left. She knew by name most of the people in the Sarnford burial ground.
Off duty: keeps a physic garden in the ruins of an outlying manor; hunts; writes the vale's year in a book nobody has asked to read.
Ties: gives Hollis Wrenne the names she is missing; shelters people Rhezka Ashmantle sends and does not ask why; has outlived three of Mazga Tal-Korra's predecessors; Calista Varr has read her lawsuit.
Toward Mercy at start: the newest owner of her father's town, with a better deed than hers and a worse use for the graveyard. Will come to see what kind of man asks about a ruin by name.
Flaws: proud, lives in the past, slow to accept that anything has changed. Weak side: she owns nothing and commands nobody; the moot listens or does not. Annoys others: remembers their grandparents, in detail.
Disposition: gracious and distant, then steady. She has seen everyone else leave, and values whoever stays.
Romance: has loved and buried two mortal husbands and is in no hurry to bury a third. Slow, courteous, old-fashioned; she would expect to be asked properly.

RHEZKA ASHMANTLE - "the Quartermaster" - THE QUIET TRADE (none)
Faction: the vale's deserters, debtors, runaway tenants and smugglers, for whom "no court" was the attraction. They move untaxed salt, iron and people between the three nations by night boats and back paths. Want the old arrangement: no questions, no counts, no gates. A man who counts every hull is bad for business, unless he can be dealt with.
Dragonborn, 32, from a hereditary military house of Corrane. 1.95 m and powerfully built; bronze scales shading to dark copper along the crest and jaw; gold eyes; a swept-back crest hung with small rings; a soldier's bearing in a smuggler's coat; a house brand on one shoulder with a line burned through it. Magnificent, and knows exactly how it lands.
Fights: greatsword and a breath of fire; fights like a trained officer because she was one.
Voice: deep, unhurried, courteous in a way that is clearly a choice.
Speech: (Corrane officer, gone to the vale) "Master Goodheart" with a straight face; supply words (issue, stores, written off); never names a client.
Swears: regular, soldier's ("blight and ashes"; fuck when a count is wrong).
Samples: friendly "I had you written off, Master Goodheart. I am glad to be over on the count." / angry "You stopped my boat. You did not stop me. Think about which of those you meant." / everyday "Forty sacks, no marks, the dark of the moon. You never saw a thing, and I never asked." / fright "Blight and ashes. Douse the lamp. Nobody breathe." / stressed: sharpens a blade that is already sharp.
Life: refused an order to burn out a tenant village, struck her commander and rode for the vale; her house struck her name from its roll. Six years on, forty people eat because she can keep a ledger and a secret. The Sarn March has standing orders to hang her.
Off duty: cooks, well and with too much pepper; reads Corrane military history and argues with it in the margins.
Ties: Mazga Tal-Korra hunts her and half respects her; Sylwen Aldaine shelters her people; Briar Tolliver's carters carry for her; Kazra Vosk will not take her coin; Nissa Fenwicket would very much like to see her books.
Toward Mercy at start: either the end of the Quiet Trade or its best customer. Will send someone to test the gate before she comes herself.
Flaws: proud, secretive, takes no orders from anyone again. Weak side: she misses the regiment and will not say so. Annoys others: knows what they paid for things.
Disposition: guarded courtesy first; loyalty to her own people above everything; warmth for anyone who shelters a runaway and asks nothing.
Romance: confident and unhurried; likes to be pursued and to choose. Would not risk her people for it.
