09N_SPELLS CINDERFORD v4 - DRAFT (planning 23 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. Replaces 09h_hearth_spells and drafts v1 to v3. Changed in v3 and v4: crystal price, growing, use. Changed in v2: mana recovery, crystals.
The ten spells are the player's picks (23 Nov 2026). Every step line, number and incantation in this file is a narrator proposal until approved, except where marked (player ruling).

1 RULES
- ONE spell per subject. Ten spells (player ruling).
- A spell's STEP is the subject's full rank. It rises by itself on promotion; spells are never upgraded separately (player ruling). The weakest step is castable at Novice (player ruling).
- Cost and speed by step: Novice 10 mana, slow (both action slots; interrupted by a hit), check -20 · Beginner 8, main action, -10 · Apprentice 7, main action, 0 · Journeyman 5, quick (offhand), +5 · Expert 4, +10 · Master 3, +15 · Grandmaster 2, +22.
- A spell does exactly its step. Each step includes everything the steps below it do, unless it replaces a number. A roll is made only when something outside the spell is uncertain (a target dodging, a guard resisting).
- Every cast is its incantation, spoken aloud by Mercy, word for word, and heard by everyone in earshot. Never silent, never skipped in narration.
- Necromancy is painless (player ruling).
- Maximum mana: per the ledger (now 32). Mana returns in full after a night's sleep (player ruling). [PROPOSED: a night's sleep is at least 6 hours unbroken.]
- Raising minions is a rite with its own costs: 05m_minions. It is not one of these spells.
- HEALING is not one of these spells either: see section 3.

2 THE TEN SPELLS

### BONE SPEAR
- Subject: Python
- Incantation: "This will hurt you more than me."
- Novice: One shard at one target within 15 m. Wounds foes up to Danger 10. Needs a bone in hand or underfoot.
- Beginner: 30 m. Wounds foes up to Danger 25.
- Apprentice: Goes through a shield or leather. The target is pinned: it loses its next main action.
- Journeyman: 60 m. Wounds foes up to Danger 40. No bone needed.
- Expert: Passes through the first target and strikes a second in line.
- Master: 100 m. Wounds foes up to Danger 60.
- Grandmaster: The spear comes back and strikes again each exchange until the target is down or Mercy lets it go.
- Notes: The single hard blow. Bone Volley is the many.

### GRAVE FOG
- Subject: Linux/Bash
- Incantation: "Nothing to see here."
- Novice: Cold mist 10 m across round a point within 30 m, for ten minutes. Sight inside is 3 m. His minions are not hindered.
- Beginner: 30 m across, for an hour. Mercy sees through it too.
- Apprentice: 100 m across (a bridge, the neck gate), until the next dawn or dusk. The living inside are chilled: -5.
- Journeyman: Moves with him or with one named minion. Muffles sound.
- Expert: The whole Tongue, for a day. The living inside lose their bearings (a check to go where they mean to).
- Master: The Tongue and the banks, for 3 days. The living inside take -10. Arrows shot in are shot blind.
- Grandmaster: Stands over his land whenever he wishes. The living who enter unbidden walk out where they came in.
- Notes: Hides and hinders; harms nobody by itself.

### KNIT BONE
- Subject: Git/Gerrit
- Incantation: "Hold still. You won't feel a thing."
- Novice: One damaged minion he touches is whole at once.
- Beginner: One damaged minion within 10 m; or one broken plain thing of bone or wood.
- Apprentice: A minion destroyed within the last minute stands up again. Once per fight.
- Journeyman: Up to eight minions in sight.
- Expert: A destroyed minion whose remains he holds returns in a day, not a week.
- Master: Every minion of his within 100 m, the minute's destroyed included.
- Grandmaster: Destroyed minions on his land rise at the next dusk.
- Notes: Mends dead things only. It never heals the living: that is Healing, and it hurts.

### DEAD MAN'S EYES
- Subject: Cybersecurity
- Incantation: "Let me see that."
- Novice: Sees through the eyes of one minion within 100 m for ten minutes. His own body is blind and still meanwhile.
- Beginner: Sees and hears. 1 km. An hour.
- Apprentice: Any minion on his land. He keeps his own senses as well.
- Journeyman: Two minions at once, anywhere in the vale. Darkness is no bar.
- Expert: Speaks through its mouth.
- Master: Any minion of his, anywhere. He may cast Bone Spear, Grave Fog or Dread Presence from where it stands, at the Novice step.
- Grandmaster: Sees through all of them as he wishes, without casting.
- Notes: Shows what the minion could see; a mindless minion notices nothing by itself.

### SUMMON MOUNT
- Subject: Docker/K8s
- Incantation: "Somebody get me a horse. Anybody's."
- Novice: A spectral steed for an hour, for him alone, at a horse's pace, on his land.
- Beginner: Until the next dawn or dusk, anywhere. Tireless: 80 km.
- Apprentice: A day and a night. Carries him and one more, or 150 kg. Sure on mud and ice.
- Journeyman: Crosses water. Twice a horse's pace.
- Expert: Mounts for up to eight riders.
- Master: Rides the air above the trees. 3 days.
- Grandmaster: Comes at a thought, with no casting; a mount for every death knight when he calls.
- Notes: Not a minion: outside the caps, cannot fight, gone when the time ends.

### BRACE
- Subject: IaC
- Incantation: "It'll hold. It's had worse."
- Novice: One door, gate or beam within 10 m holds against one person forcing it, or one hard blow, for ten minutes.
- Beginner: A gate or 10 m of wall for an hour. Forcing it is Hard.
- Apprentice: One whole structure (a bridge, a gatehouse, a wall section) for a night. It stands through storm and flood, and ordinary fire will not catch on it.
- Journeyman: A day. Danger 25 attackers lose an exchange at each attempt. Breaking in needs a ram or Danger 40.
- Expert: Two structures, or the whole neck wall, for 3 days.
- Master: A week. Only siege engines or magic break it.
- Grandmaster: While he lives and stands on the Tongue, nothing he has braced falls.
- Notes: Holds what is there; repairs nothing. A breach stays a breach.

### DREAD PRESENCE
- Subject: Azure
- Incantation: "Let's be reasonable."
- Novice: For a minute, the living within 10 m who can see him: an ordinary person must pass a check to defy him to his face. Armed and angry people are not stopped.
- Beginner: 20 m, ten minutes. Beasts will not come near. A trained fighter must pass a check to strike first.
- Apprentice: An hour. May be laid on one named minion in his stead.
- Journeyman: 50 m. Those who fail back away. A crowd breaks up.
- Expert: Covers a bridge or the gate for as long as he stands there. Veterans and the Called must check too.
- Master: Everyone on his land who can see him. A formed company halts for one exchange.
- Grandmaster: His name does it: whoever has felt it once must check again whenever they mean to cross him, anywhere.
- Notes: Fear, not agreement. It makes refusing harder; it makes nobody pay. The cowed remember it.

### READ INTENT
- Subject: AI
- Incantation: "What seems to be the trouble?"
- Novice: One person within 5 m, in conversation: the thing they want most right now, as a feeling.
- Beginner: What they want or what they fear, in a plain phrase.
- Apprentice: 20 m, no conversation needed. Whether they mean him harm today.
- Journeyman: What they are hiding, in general terms (cargo, name, errand). Three people.
- Expert: A whole party at once: who leads, who would break, who could be bought.
- Master: At a distance, through a minion's eyes. What they intend over the coming days.
- Grandmaster: Nobody standing on his land is a stranger: he knows what each came for.
- Notes: Reads the living, not the truth of what they say. The guarded and the Called may resist (hidden check).

### BONE VOLLEY
- Subject: CI/CD
- Incantation: "Next!"
- Novice: Three shards at one target within 15 m; harm counts once. Wounds foes up to Danger 10.
- Beginner: Two targets within 30 m, at Hard; all must succeed.
- Apprentice: Three targets. Wounds foes up to Danger 25.
- Journeyman: Six targets, each rolled separately, so one miss does not spoil the rest.
- Expert: Eight targets within 60 m, at Normal. Wounds foes up to Danger 40.
- Master: Twelve targets. Wounds foes up to Danger 60. A struck foe is pinned (loses its next main action).
- Grandmaster: Twelve at Normal within 100 m; each hit also staggers (-10 on that target's next action).
- Notes: Needs bones in reach below Journeyman. His minions are never hit.

### HAUNT
- Subject: Gravesong
- Incantation: "I'll be seeing you."
- Novice: A spirit follows one person he has seen today, for an hour. He knows their direction while they are within 1 km.
- Beginner: Until the next dawn. Direction and rough distance out to 5 km, and whether they are moving, still or asleep.
- Apprentice: 3 days, to the edges of the vale (about 15 km). Two people. If he wishes, the haunted sleep badly: -5 on all checks next day.
- Journeyman: A week, as far as the nearest towns (about 50 km). How many are with them, and an outline of whom they met.
- Expert: Once a day a glimpse of where they are. The spirit may show itself to them if he wishes.
- Master: A month, any distance along the three roads and rivers. A minute of sight and hearing each day. Three people.
- Grandmaster: Everyone who has crossed without paying, always. Once a night, one sentence in his voice.
- Notes: For those who rode through without paying, and for those sent to watch him.

3 HEALING AND RESURRECTION (player rulings; a separate passive skill, tied to no rank and never improving with one)
- HEALING: costs no mana. Costs pain: his hands shake, he feels the patient's pain where the patient felt it, his eyes bleed. He would really rather not. [PROPOSED: one health level restored per use; -10 on all his checks for an hour afterwards.]
- RESURRECTION: true life, the person's own mind returned; not undeath. Once per day. He can cast nothing else that day. Needs a magical crystal. The body must be less than a week old. About ten living healers can do it.
- CRYSTALS (player ruling): they are grown, not mined. Each of the three nations has a source. Growing one takes a lot of mana. One crystal is used up by one resurrection. [PROPOSED: the last sentence.]
- PRICE (player ruling): a crystal bought from one of the nations' sources costs 2,000 coins.
- GROWING (player rulings): Mercy's liches grow one crystal a month, in a building made for it, and he can have only one such building (07_necropolis, Crystal Vault). Either one lich gives all her mana to it every day, or all the liches give a part of theirs every day.
- Only Mercy can use a crystal grown there. They are not sold (player ruling).
- Who sells crystals in each nation: NOT SET.

4 CASTABLE NOW (ledger v10 ranks)
- Beginner step, 8 mana: Grave Fog, Knit Bone, Summon Mount, Read Intent.
- Novice step, 10 mana: Bone Spear, Dead Man's Eyes, Brace, Dread Presence, Bone Volley, Haunt.

END OF SPELLS v4
