07B_PRICES CINDERFORD v1 - DRAFT (planning 24 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. A price guide for everything that is not a necropolis building: works for the living, repairs, wages, goods, beasts, boats, arms, rents.
Every figure is a narrator proposal until approved. Figures agree with 10_world 7, 07_necropolis and 05m_minions; where they differ, those files win and this one is corrected.

1 HOW TO READ A PRICE
- A work is priced as in 07_necropolis: LD (one adult working one day; "skilled" needs a craftsman or a directed gravewright, or takes double) · loads of timber, stone, thatch · coin (c) for iron and bought goods.
- His own dead cost nothing a day: skeletons give 2 unskilled LD each, gravewrights 2 skilled LD each under direction (05m). Materials can be cut and dragged by them: timber and thatch 1 LD a load, stone 2 loads per LD from the ruins.
- HIRED AND BOUGHT instead: an unskilled LD 3c and a skilled LD 7c, fed; timber 4c a load, stone 3c a load, thatch 3c a load, delivered to the crossing. The "all hired" figure after each work is the whole thing done that way, to the nearest 10. It is the most it should cost.
- Living workers come only if they are willing to work here, and say what they want for it. A price is not a promise that anyone takes the job.
- Works marked BANK stand on the bank land, where the living may be. Works with no mark may stand on either; on the Tongue only his own use them.
- A work that needs a keeper (smith, brewer, innkeeper, miller) does nothing until a living person agrees to keep it. Terms are made in play; section 8 gives usual rents.
- Frozen ground stops digging, mortar and clay work (about December to February). Costs are quoted before commitment; nothing is begun or bought automatically.

4 WAGES (by the day, 10_world 7)
- Labourer 2 to 3. Carter with his own cart and team 6. Boatman 3.
- Skilled craftsman (carpenter, mason, smith, thatcher) 5 to 8. Master builder or engineer who can plan a tower or a bridge 12.
- Clerk who reads and counts 5. Cook 3. Innkeeper or steward: a share, section 8.
- Hired guard 4 and food. A hired company charges the same a head, more for officers, by written contract; the fee is haggled (06_cast, the Ninth Article).
- Food for one working adult: 1 a day if bought.

7 ARMS AND KIT

8 RENTS, FEES AND SHARES (what a living keeper or user usually pays the owner; all are bargained in play)
- Market stall: 1 a trading day. Camp pitch: 1 a night (toll card).
- Cottage: 8 a month. Bunk hall bed: 1 a night.
- Alehouse: 40 a month. Tavern with rooms: 150 a month, or a fifth of the takings. Smithy: 20 a month. Brewhouse: 30 a month. Mill: a sixteenth of what is ground.
- Warehouse: 1 per cartload per week. Weigh-house: 1 a cart. Crane: 5 a barge. Stable: 1 a beast a night. Boat slip: 2 a day.
- A keeper who is asked to live beside the dead may want easier terms, or none at all.
