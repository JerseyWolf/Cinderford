07_BUILDINGS CINDERFORD v1 - DRAFT (planning 23 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Replaces 07_buildings v0.

1 RULES
- Three buildings per fire skill, thirty in all (player Q67).
- UNLOCK: building 1 opens at subject rank Novice, building 2 at Beginner, building 3 at Apprentice.
- UPGRADES: every later rank opens the next level of every building of that skill already unlocked. Levels are built in order.
- ADD-ONS: small improvements that can be added to a standing building at any time for resources. No rank needed. One of each per building unless it says otherwise.
- People build (settlers, hired hands), and from the Wisp Crew spell and Structure powers, the fire helps. Nobody builds on command.
- HEARTH-BOUND: a building's written effect works while it stands inside ACT range (now 10 m) and the fire is Low or better. Outside it, it is an ordinary building with its capacity and shelter only. Exception: buildings that are by nature out on the roads (Outpost Hearths, Road-Fires, Distance Stones and Waystones, Ford Works, Burial Ground, Beacon Cairn) work where they stand.
- Effects change what is possible or what a success achieves, never a chance, unless the line says so.
- COSTS: LD = one adult working one day. "skilled" needs a craftsman directing, or it takes double. Timber load = 1 LD to fell and drag. Stone = 2 loads per LD from the ruins (about 80 loads there). Thatch load = 1 LD from the river meadows. Iron and bought goods in coins (c).
- Costs are quoted before commitment. Nothing is begun or bought automatically. Frozen ground (about December to February) stops digging and clay work. Repair after damage costs a share of the original.
- Levels written in full: those reachable at Novice, Beginner and Apprentice. Later levels are named only and are costed when a subject nears Journeyman.

2 THE THIRTY BUILDINGS

== FLAME (Python) ==
1 HEARTH
- Level 1 (Novice) · Ring Reset: 2 LD · 3 stone. Ordinary rain and wind alone cannot push the fire below Flickering. Cooks for 10.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Raised Hearth: 8 LD · 10 stone · iron 12c. Cooks for 30. Banked coals hold: fuel starvation takes 48 hours, not 24.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Hearth House: 30 LD skilled · 12 timber · 6 thatch · iron 20c. Weather cannot harm the fire. Act range is measured from its walls.
- Later levels: great hearth; oath-stone setting.
- Add-ons: Spit and pot crane (iron 6c): roasts for 20. Ash pit (1 LD): lye and potash for soap and sale.
2 BAKEHOUSE AND KITCHEN
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Clay Oven: 6 LD · 2 stone · 1 timber. Bread for 20 a day. Meals sell for more.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Kitchen: 35 LD skilled · 14 timber · 8 stone · 6 thatch · iron 15c. Feeds 60. Travel-bread to sell to convoys.
- Later levels: brewhouse; second oven range.
- Add-ons: Smoke rack (2 LD · 1 timber): meat and fish keep for months. Salt box and spice shelf (10c).
3 FORGE
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Smithy: 20 LD skilled · 6 timber · 6 stone · anvil and tools 120c · needs a smith. Shoeing, nails, repairs on site. Iron for site jobs costs half.
- Later levels: bloomery; armourer's bench.
- Add-ons: Quench trough (1 LD). Nail header (8c): the site makes its own nails.

== GROUND (Linux/Bash) ==
1 YARD AND WELL
- Level 1 (Novice) · Cleared Yard: 4 LD. Dry ground for 4 tents or 2 carts. A latrine downwind.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Well Cleared: 10 LD · 2 stone · rope and bucket 4c. Clean water on site.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Paved Yard: 30 LD · 20 stone. No mud. Carts turn. Less sickness.
- Later levels: cistern; drained cart road.
- Add-ons: Windlass (1 LD · iron 3c). Horse trough (1 LD · 1 timber).
2 WOODSTORE AND FUEL YARD
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Woodstore: 4 LD · 3 timber · 1 thatch. 30 days of dry fuel. Dry fuel fed by a person gives +2 mana that hour, not +1.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Fuel Yard: 18 LD · 6 timber. 90 days of fuel. Sawpit: boards cut on site. Charcoal clamp: charcoal for the forge and for sale.
- Later levels: coppice plot; charcoal burners' camp.
- Add-ons: Chopping block and two axes (12c). Kindling loft (1 LD).
3 GARDEN AND PADDOCK
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Garden and Paddock: 20 LD · 6 timber · seed 10c. From the second season: greens and roots for 6. Grazing for 8 beasts.
- Later levels: orchard; fishpond and weir; root cellar.
- Add-ons: Beehives (15c). Hen run (2 LD · 1 timber · hens 6c).

== MENDING (Git/Gerrit) ==
1 SICKBED
- Level 1 (Novice) · Warm Corner: 1 LD · 1 timber. One sick or hurt person rests properly by the fire.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Sickbed: 5 LD · 2 timber · linen 10c · needs a roof. Two beds. Wounds tended daily here do not fester.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Infirmary: 40 LD skilled · 16 timber · 8 thatch · iron 12c · linen 25c. Four beds behind their own door. Sickness kept here does not spread.
- Later levels: six-bed ward; bone-setter's frame; birthing room.
- Add-ons: Bandage chest (6c). Screens (1 LD).
2 STILLROOM
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Herb Shelf: 3 LD · 1 timber · pots 8c · needs a roof. Common remedies on hand: a Bruised wound heals a day sooner.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Stillroom: 25 LD skilled · 6 timber · glass and copper 60c · needs a herbwife or healer. Remedies made for use and sale. Bruised heals in half the time.
- Later levels: apothecary garden; distillery.
- Add-ons: Mortar and scales (9c). Drying rack (1 LD).
3 BATHHOUSE
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Tub House: 25 LD · 10 timber · 4 stone · 4 thatch · two tubs 20c. Hot water from the hearth stones. Lice and skin sickness drop. Exhaustion clears in an evening. Guests pay for it.
- Later levels: sweat room; laundry.
- Add-ons: Laundry copper (18c). Sweat bench (2 LD · 1 timber).

== VIGIL / VEIL (Cybersecurity) ==
1 HEDGE
- Level 1 (Novice) · Thorn Hedge: 3 LD. Cut blackthorn and strung bells. Nothing bigger than a fox comes in at night without noise.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Pale: 25 LD · 20 timber · iron 10c. Chest-high split timber. Keeps out beasts and casual thieves.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Palisade: 120 LD skilled · 90 timber · iron 40c. 3 m stakes and a fighting step. A dozen defenders can hold against Danger 25 raiders. The Peace forbids fortification: this is a political act.
- Later levels: ditch and bank; hidden postern.
- Add-ons: Trip lines (2 LD). Live thorn planted (3 LD; grown in three years).
2 GATE AND WATCH POST
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Barred Gate and Stand: 8 LD · 5 timber · iron 8c. Everyone enters in view of the fire. A watcher sees 1 km of each road by day.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Gatehouse: 45 LD skilled · 25 timber · iron 20c. A 5 m platform: 2 km of each road. The gate holds a rush for several exchanges.
- Later levels: second gate; murder-hole (see Palisade warning).
- Add-ons: Gate bell (5c). Spy slit (1 LD).
3 CELLAR AND STRONGROOM
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Cellar: 30 LD · 8 timber · 6 stone · lock 15c. Cool store: food keeps weeks longer. Hides four people or a cartload. Blind Eye cast on it lasts twice as long.
- Later levels: vault; escape tunnel.
- Add-ons: False wall (4 LD · 2 timber). Strongbox (40c).

== EMBERS (Docker/K8s) ==
1 LANTERN ROOM
- Level 1 (Novice) · Lantern Hooks: 2 LD · 2 timber · iron 6c. A hook at each road mouth for a lit lantern.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Lamp Shelf: 2 LD · four horn lanterns 12c · needs a roof. A plain ember carried in a house lantern lasts twice as long.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Lamp Room: 12 LD skilled · 4 timber · twelve lanterns 36c. One more ember may be out at a time than rank allows.
- Later levels: ember vault; glass lamps.
- Add-ons: Storm lantern (6c): the ember survives rain. Clay ember pots (1 LD).
2 STABLE AND CART SHED
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Cart Shed and Byre: 30 LD · 22 timber · 8 thatch · iron 12c. Two carts, six beasts, hay loft. Carters choose to stop. Sleeps 2.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Stable: 80 LD skilled · 50 timber · 20 thatch · iron 40c. Ten stalls and a tack room. Remounts can be kept.
- Later levels: wagon yard; boat landing.
- Add-ons: Hitching rail and trough (2 LD · 2 timber). Farrier's stand (3 LD; needs the Forge).
