3 OUTPOST HEARTHS
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Way-Hearth: 6 LD · 4 stone · 1 timber each. A ringed hearth and woodbox 1 km out on a road. An ember left there keeps without a carrier. Travellers camp at it.
- Later levels: way-huts; a chain of hearths to the vale's edge.
- Add-ons: Shelter roof (4 LD · 3 timber · 1 thatch). Marker cairn (1 LD).

== STRUCTURE (IaC) ==
1 THE HOUSE
- Level 1 (Novice) · Lean-to: 6 LD · 4 timber · 2 thatch (or a canvas). A dry roof over the cook-pot and a table for 6. Sleeps 2. The first building.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Longhouse: 90 LD skilled · 40 timber · 25 thatch · iron 30c. One room, 5 by 9 m, with a door that shuts. Room for 20. Sleeps 8. People can winter here.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Hall and Loft: 70 LD skilled · 30 timber · 15 thatch · iron 25c. Room for 40. A sleeping loft: sleeps 6 more.
- Later levels: second wing; stone footing and tile; a proper inn.
- Add-ons: Shutters (2 LD · iron 4c). Plank floor (8 LD · boards 25c). Door bar (iron 3c).
2 GUEST ROOMS
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Bunk Row: 8 LD · 6 timber · 2 thatch. Four bunks behind a curtain against any wall. Sleeps 4.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Room Wing: 80 LD skilled · 35 timber · 15 thatch · iron 30c. Four rooms with doors. Sleeps 8. Paying guests who want a door; settlers with a place of their own.
- Later levels: upper storey; a best chamber.
- Add-ons: Straw ticks and blankets (3c a bed). Door locks (5c each).
3 CARPENTER'S WORKSHOP
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Workshop: 15 LD · 8 timber · 3 thatch · tools 60c · best with a carpenter. Skilled jobs on site take three quarters of their labour-days. Carts and furniture repaired.
- Later levels: wheelwright's shop; timber frame yard.
- Add-ons: Pole lathe (2 LD · iron 4c). Shingle horse (1 LD).

== PRESENCE (Azure) ==
1 LONG TABLE
- Level 1 (Novice) · Bench: 1 LD · 1 timber. A split-log bench for 6 by the pit. Strangers sit down instead of standing off.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · The Long Table: 6 LD · 4 timber · iron 4c · needs a roof. Sixteen at one table. Strangers eat together. Guest-right is understood to cover the whole table.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Common Room: 25 LD skilled · 10 timber · iron 10c · needs the Longhouse. Settles, a high table, thirty seats. Feasts. A quarrel at table is everyone's business.
- Later levels: feast hall; moot-ring where disputes are heard.
- Add-ons: Tally board (1 LD). House sign (2 LD · paint 5c): a name on the road.
2 OFFERING STONE
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Offering Stone: 2 LD · 1 stone. A flat stone with a carved bowl by the pit. People leave offerings more often.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Hearth Niche: 10 LD skilled · 3 stone · 1 timber · 10c. A bread shelf and lamp. Travellers of every faith leave something. An offering gives +4 mana, not +3.
- Later levels: guest-shrine; first-loaf oven.
- Add-ons: Alms box (6c): coin offerings kept for the house. First-crust basket (1c).
3 MARKET PITCH
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Market Pitch: 35 LD · 12 timber · 10 stone · scales and weights 50c · needs the Cleared Yard. Six stall frames and a weigh-beam. Trading on Tuesdays and Saturdays. The house takes stall fees.
- Later levels: Tally Day fair; covered market.
- Add-ons: Awnings (cloth 12c). Notice board (1 LD).

== INSIGHT (AI) ==
1 PERCH AND ROOST
- Level 1 (Novice) · Perch Post: 1 LD · 1 timber. Gary sits in the smoke, sees faces and passes for a tame bird.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Roost Box: 3 LD · 1 timber · iron 2c · needs a roof. Gary stays out through rain and frost unremarked and sees who comes before they reach the hedge.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Mews Loft: 10 LD · 4 timber · iron 4c. Three openings: Gary watches all three roads at once and can carry a letter unnoticed.
- Later levels: carved guardian post; owl-tower.
- Add-ons: Message tube and jesses (3c). Mouse run (1 LD).
2 SNUG
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Settle and Screen: 3 LD · 2 timber. The warmest corner. Quiet talk gathers inside act range.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Snug Room: 20 LD skilled · 8 timber · iron 8c. A small room with a door and a hatch to the hearth. Private meetings. Ordinary speech in it is heard as if at the pit.
- Later levels: council room; envoy's chamber.
- Add-ons: A good chair (6c). Dice and board (4c): people linger and talk.
3 TALLY ROOM
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Tally Room: 12 LD skilled · 4 timber · paper and ink 30c · needs someone who writes. Guest-book, debts and regulars recorded. Letters held for travellers, who come back for them. The fire can read what is written inside act range.
- Later levels: archive; map room.
- Add-ons: Seal and wax (8c). Map board (2 LD).

== SPARKS (CI/CD) ==
1 BEACON
- Level 1 (Novice) · Fire-Basket: 2 LD · 1 timber · iron 10c. An iron cresset on a 4 m pole. Seen 3 km down each road on a clear night.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Beacon Cairn: 12 LD · 14 stone. On the rise to the south. Seen from both rims and from the ferry 9 km downstream.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Beacon Tower: 60 LD skilled · 30 timber · iron 20c. 8 m of timber. Seen 15 km off.
- Later levels: stone tower; mirror and lens.
- Add-ons: Coloured smoke powders (10c). Flashing shutter (2 LD · iron 3c).
2 ROAD-FIRES
- Level 1 (Beginner) · One Road-Fire: 5 LD · 2 timber · iron 10c each. A cresset and woodbox 1 km out on one road, kept by arrangement.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Three Road-Fires: 15 LD · 6 timber · iron 30c · someone to tend each. Warning reaches the crossroads faster than a runner.
- Later levels: a chain to the vale's edge.
- Add-ons: A horn at each (4c).
3 SIGNAL MAST
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Signal Mast: 15 LD skilled · 3 timber · rope and cloth 20c. Day flags and night lights in an agreed code. Convoys know whether the ford is open before they commit.
- Later levels: code shared with the towns.
- Add-ons: Code sheets for regulars (5c each).

== ROADS (Gravesong) ==
1 SIGNPOST AND WAYSTONES
- Level 1 (Novice) · Signpost Raised: 3 LD · 1 timber. Three arms re-cut, the milestone reset. The place has a name on a board again.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Distance Stones: 9 LD · 3 stone. A stone 1 km out on each road.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Waystones: 60 LD skilled · 15 stone. A carved stone each kilometre for 5 km on all three roads. Strangers arrive knowing what is here.
- Later levels: stones to the vale's edge; a road book.
- Add-ons: Painted hand (2c). Bench at the post (1 LD · 1 timber).
2 FORD WORKS
- Level 1 (Beginner) · Depth Posts and Rope: 8 LD · 4 timber · rope 8c. Anyone can see whether the ford will take a cart. Fewer drownings.
- Level 2 (Apprentice) · Ferry Raft: 40 LD skilled · 14 timber · rope and iron 40c. A raft on a hauling line: people and small loads cross in flood weeks.
- Later levels: causeway; timber bridge (every neighbour will have a view).
- Add-ons: Lantern on the far post (6c). Stepping stones for low water (4 LD · 6 stone).
3 BURIAL GROUND AND SHRINE
- Level 1 (Apprentice) · Burial Ground Tended: 20 LD · 6 timber · 2 stone. Cleared, markers straightened, a lych-gate and a Ferrywoman's post. Kin notice. Burial-priests stop here.
- Later levels: chapel of the Ferrywoman; ossuary.
- Add-ons: Name board for the war dead (3 LD). Yew planted (1 LD).
