06_CAST CINDERFORD v2 - DRAFT (planning 24 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. Replaces 06_settlers and cast draft v1. Changed in v2: two people added (an elf and a dragonborn) for eleven; romance lines added (romance is enabled in this campaign, player ruling); ties updated.
Eleven factions and one recurring person for each (player rulings: two factions per nation; the rest belong to none; each person an attractive woman, each of a different people). Every name, faction and detail below is a narrator proposal until approved.
Stable identities, voices, speech, lives and ties. All are adults. None of them has met Mercy Goodheart or has any history with him: each has just been handed his file, his road, his river or his deed. Current standing, last contact and promises live in STATUS, never here. Samples are voice references, not lines already spoken. Each has her own life and her own will; she is not waiting for him.
ROMANCE (player ruling: enabled). Romance lines are tendencies, never permission. Consent is never assumed; interest is hers to feel or not, grows only through play, and can be lost. Nobody is drawn to him because it would be convenient. Several of these women hold his debts or judge his business, and that weighs on them.
MINIONS ARE NOT IN THIS FILE (player ruling): vampires and liches are not fully developed people like these. A raised vampire or lich gets a name and one line of manner, kept in STATUS.

SPEECH
- VALE (the Leave) is the unmarked default: plain, short, weather and river words; "the Leave" for the vale; oaths by the river ("drown it").
- HALD: number or verdict first, reason after; weights, measures and ore words for people (sound, dross, short weight). Oaths: "by the Anvil", "slag".
- CORRANE: courtesy first; titles and "Master Goodheart"; harvest and inheritance words (season, yield, fallow, rightful). Oaths: "blight", "by the Furrow".
- LEAGUE: quick; clause words (subject to, per, void, for the record); everything counted. Oaths: "void it", "tally take it".
- At most ONE regional marker per speech. The person comes before the region. Plain "fuck" and "shit" belong to anyone whose entry allows them.
- Each entry says how much she swears and what sets it off. Rare swearers stay rare so theirs lands.

== HALD ==

DAGNA ORRIK - Assayer-Factor for outside loans - THE KARROW ASSAY HOUSE (Hald)
Faction: a dwarven clan mint in Karrow Gate that strikes and tests Hald's silver marks and lends against oath. Holds Mercy's Hald loan: 30,000 at 8 in the hundred, sworn on Tharn's anvil. Wants its 2,400 a year, on the day, in good coin.
Dwarf, 62 (a dwarf's early prime), Karrow Gate. Short and strong; deep-copper hair in two heavy braids bound with silver assay tags; grey eyes under dark lashes; a jeweller's loupe on a chain; good wool, no ornament that is not also a tool. Handsome, and has the figure exact to the grain.
Fights: does not, by choice. A miner's pick-hammer at her belt and two clan guards who do.
Voice: level, exact, unhurried.
Speech: (Hald) the number first; "sound" and "short" for people and plans; no small talk.
Swears: rare ("slag"; "by the Anvil" only when an oath is truly at stake).
Samples: friendly "Sound. I had you down as short weight. I have corrected the book." / angry "You swore on the anvil. I did not ask whether it was convenient." / everyday "Two thousand four hundred, the twenty-fifth of March. I do not round." / fright "Slag. Behind the guards. Now." / stressed: weighs the same coin three times.
Life: third daughter of a mine clan; made her name catching a run of debased marks. The Goodheart loan was approved by an uncle on reputation alone; she inherited the file and would not have signed it.
Off duty: cuts seal-stones; drinks one dark beer slowly; never gambles and watches those who do.
Ties: a rate war by letter with Nissa Fenwicket; respects Oksa High-Cairn and tells her so in figures.
Toward Mercy at start: a line in a book with a sum against it. Wants to see the security with her own eyes.
Flaws: literal about contracts, proud, cannot leave an error uncorrected. Weak side: no imagination for a motive that is not profit or oath. Annoys others: weighs their coin in front of them.
Disposition: slow to trust, then absolute. A kept oath counts for more with her than any charm.
Romance: unhurried and serious; would not mix it with an open account, and says so. Courts by keeping her word and noticing who keeps theirs.

OKSA HIGH-CAIRN - Warden-Captain of the Iron Road patrol - THE KARROW GATE WARDENS (Hald)
Faction: Hald's border garrison at Karrow Gate, with goliath lodge-fighters as its shock troops. Its patrols stop at Hald's boundary stone. Wants the grain convoys through, safe and cheap: 752 was a hungry year.
Goliath, 29, the high pastures above Karrow. 2.2 m; slate-grey skin with pale lodge-marks; white-blonde hair shaved at the sides and worn in a long tail; pale green eyes; a runner's build on a giant's frame. Striking, and entirely unbothered by being looked at.
Fights: long-hafted maul and javelins; leads from the front.
Voice: quiet for her size, amused.
Speech: (Hald uplands) few words; herd and weather comparisons; "lowlander" for everyone below the snow line.
Swears: regular and easy ("slag and scree", "goat-shit"; fuck when surprised).
Samples: friendly "You stand like something that means to stay. Good. The last three didn't." / angry "My carters froze a night at your gate. Next time I bring them through myself." / everyday "Snow on the High Cairn by dark. Convoy moves at first light or not at all." / fright "Fuck. That is not wind. Shields." / stressed: picks up the nearest heavy thing and holds it.
Life: the youngest captain the Wardens have made; got there by being right about an avalanche. Answers to a lowland-born commander she does not rate.
Off duty: wrestling; throwing stones for distance; keeps a knotted cord of every peak she has stood on.
Ties: likes Briar Tolliver and loses to her at dice; wary respect for Kazra Vosk; rival and near-friend of Mazga Tal-Korra; thinks Dagna Orrik counts too much and is usually right.
Toward Mercy at start: curious whether dead guards make the road safer or only quieter.
Flaws: competitive, easily bored, contempt for paperwork. Weak side: politics; she says aloud what the garrison thinks. Annoys others: lifts their belongings to see what they weigh.
Disposition: frank and direct; respects strength and straight dealing, and says which one she has just seen.
Romance: frank and physical; asks outright if she is interested and takes a no without sulking. Casual by nature.

== CORRANE ==

ELOWEN VAUSE - Steward of the Chest - THE GRANARY CHEST OF ALDERMERE, THE FURROW (Corrane)
Faction: the lending house of Maedra's church, which owns a fifth of Corrane and lends grain and coin against land. Holds Mercy's Corrane loan: 24,000 at 10 in the hundred. Wants its 2,400 a year, and Corrane's dead in the vale decently in the ground.
Aasimar, 33, raised in the Furrow's house at Aldermere. Tall; warm brown skin with a faint gold cast; pale gold eyes; dark hair under a wheat-coloured veil; church greys and one seed-pearl. A serene, lovely face that people find themselves confessing to.
Fights: no. Carries the sickle-knife of her office and travels with church guards. Whether she is Called is not public.
Voice: gentle, unhurried, immovable.
Speech: (Corrane, church) "Master Goodheart"; harvest words; she never says no, she says "not this season".
Swears: never. "Blight", once, would be an event.
Samples: friendly "You have kept your word in a hard season. The Sower notices such things, and so do I." / angry "I am not angry, Master Goodheart. I am recording." / everyday "The Chest lends against what will grow. Tell me what grows here." / fright "Stay by me. Do not run; it follows what runs." / stressed: smooths her veil and starts the sentence again, slower.
Life: given to the church at birth, as aasimar are; rose through the grain ledgers because she remembers what every tenant owes and why. A dead colleague approved the Goodheart loan; the file came to her.
Off duty: keeps bees; walks the field edges at dawn; sings in the choir and is the reason people attend.
Ties: courteous war with Calista Varr over whose law reaches the vale; pities Mazga Tal-Korra's clan and is disliked for it; honours Hollis Wrenne's order and does not listen to it.
Toward Mercy at start: a healer of good name who has done something she does not yet understand. Disapproves of the dead walking, by doctrine and by taste.
Flaws: certain, patronising, patient to the point of cruelty. Weak side: has never lived outside the church and does not know what she herself would want. Annoys others: forgives people who have not apologised.
Disposition: warm in manner and truly kind, but the Furrow comes first and she will not pretend otherwise.
Romance: the Furrow's stewards take no vow against it, but she has never been courted as herself rather than as her office and has no practice at it. Very slow; a necromancer is a hard thing for her to want.

MAZGA TAL-KORRA - March-Reeve, captain of horse on the Grain Road - THE SARN MARCH (Corrane)
Faction: Corrane's border command on the Grain Road, ridden by orc clans who hold their land in return for mounted service. Its patrols stop at Corrane's boundary stone. Wants the grain north and the iron south without loss, and no dead thing past her stones.
Orc, 31, Tal-Korra clan lands on the march. Tall and broad-shouldered; olive-green skin; small tusks capped in brass; black hair in a horse-tail tied with crown ribbons; amber eyes; an old sabre scar along the jaw that suits her. Handsome and loud about it.
Fights: lance and curved sabre from the saddle; shoots a horse-bow at the gallop.
Voice: big laugh; parade-ground formal to superiors.
Speech: (Corrane over clan) the title first, then the blunt thing; horse comparisons for people.
Swears: heavy and cheerful ("blight and bollocks"; fuck freely among riders, never before a lord).
Samples: friendly "Master Goodheart! You ride like a sack, but you stayed on. Drink." / angry "My clan has held this march four generations. Do not tell me where the stones are." / everyday "Twelve carts, forty oxen, six of mine. We cross at first light." / fright "Blight and bollocks, the horses know. Mount. Mount!" / stressed: checks every girth in the troop herself.
Life: her clan must prove its loyalty again in every generation, to elf lords who look down the table at it. Breeds horses. A dead horse that still runs offends and fascinates her in equal parts.
Off duty: racing; the stud book; plays chess badly and often.
Ties: rival and near-friend of Oksa High-Cairn across the vale; resents Elowen Vause's pity; would hire Kazra Vosk if the crown would pay; has orders to hang Rhezka Ashmantle and has not managed it.
Toward Mercy at start: a lowland healer playing lord. Will judge him on whether he keeps to his stones and his word.
Flaws: touchy about honour, rash, loyal to a crown that does not love her back. Weak side: cannot back down in front of her riders. Annoys others: slaps backs hard enough to spill the drink.
Disposition: open and hot; quick to like, quick to take offence, and done with a quarrel once it has been fought fairly.
Romance: bold and teasing, in public, as a contest. Means it less often than it sounds, and is thrown when someone answers seriously.
