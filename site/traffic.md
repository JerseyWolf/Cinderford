08_TRAFFIC CINDERFORD v1 - DRAFT (planning 24 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC for now: there are no hidden pages in the first sessions (player ruling), so the player may read these tables. No tables in the layout sense. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. Replaces 08_arrivals and gm/arrival_tables.
Every number here is a narrator proposal until approved, except where marked (player ruling). All rolls in this file are hidden, honest, first roll stands (02_rules 4.4).

1 RULES
- Traffic is the world going about its business. It does not come for Mercy and it does not wait for him.
- Each day of play, or each day of a skip, roll how many parties reach the crossing by road (section 2) and by river (section 3), then what each is (section 4). Narrate only what he could see or be told.
- A TOLL CAN ONLY BE TAKEN WHERE SOMEONE CAN ASK FOR IT. By road: at a manned gate or bar on the crossing lane. By river: only if a boat can be stopped, which needs the Landing and someone afloat, or the Chain Booms (07_necropolis, River Works). With nobody at the bar, people cross and pay nothing. On Day 1 nothing is manned.
- One toll per crossing of the land, at the first bar reached. A cart's toll covers the cart, its team and its crew. Riders, walkers and beasts travelling with it pay their own.
- Nobody pays because it is convenient. Section 5 says how a party decides.
- A party that reaches the crossing after mid-afternoon camps on a bank and crosses in the morning (10_world 6).

2 ROAD PARTIES PER DAY (dates per 10_world 6)
- 15 March to 30 April, thaw: d3-1.
- May and June: d4+1.
- July and August: d6+2.
- September and October, harvest: d6+4.
- November: d3.
- December to 14 March: one party on a d100 roll of 40 or less; otherwise none. In a blizzard, none.
- Feast days (10_world 11): Roads' Opening and Tally Day double the day's number.
- Which road each came by: d6: 1-2 Iron Road (from Hald, arriving at the neck gate) · 3-4 Grain Road (from Corrane, at the South Bridge) · 5-6 Tow Road (from the League, at the North Bridge). Where it is going: one of the other two, evens.

3 BOATS PER DAY
- 15 March to 30 April: one boat on a d100 roll of 30 or less (the Upper Sarn is in flood).
- May and June: d3-1. July and August: d3. September and October: d4. November: d2-1.
- December to 14 March: none while the rivers are frozen or running ice.
- Each boat: d6: 1-4 flat boat (toll 10; crew 2 to 4) · 5-6 laden barge (toll 20; crew 4 to 8, often 1 or 2 guards). Coming down from Hald or Corrane, or up from the League, evens.

4 WHAT A ROAD PARTY IS (d100)
- 01-18 ON FOOT: d6 people. Pedlar, pilgrims, a messenger, labourers looking for work, a family moving. Toll 1 each. No guards.
- 19-28 RIDERS: d3 riders. A courier, a factor, a gentleman and servant. Toll 2 each.
- 29-52 CARTS: d3 carts, a carter and a boy to each. Guards: d3-1. Toll 5 a cart.
- 53-64 DROVERS: 2d6 x 10 head of cattle, sheep or swine; 2 to 5 drovers and their dogs. Toll 1 per 5 head.
- 65-70 PACK TRAIN: 2d5+2 pack beasts, 2 to 4 people. Guards: d3-1. Toll 2 a beast.
- 71-86 SMALL CONVOY: d6+3 carts under one master. Guards: d6, at Danger 10. Toll 5 a cart.
- 87-94 LARGE CONVOY (September and October only; in other months read as a small convoy): 4d8+8 carts and wagons, grain north or iron and salt south. Guards: 2d6+6, at Danger 25 together, often a hired company under its own officer. Toll 5 a cart, 8 a heavy wagon.
- 95-97 ON BUSINESS: d4+1 people with a reason beyond trade: a clerk with a letter, a burial-priest, a bank's agent, a surveyor, an envoy's party. Some will argue that their office excuses them.
- 98-00 TROUBLE: not customers. d6: 1-2 goblins from the north rim, thieving or selling furs · 3 deserters or robbers watching the camp grounds · 4 a beast down from the rims (wolves; in winter worse) · 5 someone fleeing something, asking to be let through or hidden · 6 something from the War Meadow, or the river in flood.
- Most merchants travel with at least a few guards (player ruling). A lone cart with none is poor, local or foolish.
- Colour, names and what they talk about are fresh each time (01_narration 4). A party is somebody: where from, where to, carrying what, in what temper.

5 WILL THEY PAY
First see what stands at the bar, as the party sees it: THE SHOW.
- NOTHING: no bar, or nobody at it. They cross.
- A MAN IN THE ROAD: someone asking, with no gate or bar to shut.
- A BAR HELD: a gate or bar that shuts, and guards at it. Compare the guards' Danger tier (05m 1) with the party's.
Then one hidden roll for the party's leader. CHANCE TO PAY THE ASKED TOLL WITHOUT ARGUMENT = 50, and:
- the bar's guards outmatch the party's: +20. The party outmatches the bar: -30.
- only a man in the road: -20.
- the rates are painted on a toll board, and are the same for everyone: +10.
- WORD (section 6): +0 at Unknown, +5 Rumour, +15 Known, +25 Established.
- a season rate agreed with the Axle and Oar, for its members: +20.
- first time this party has seen the dead walk: -10. Their beasts will not pass the dead: -10 until the guards stand back or are upwind.
- the toll asked is more than twice the toll card (02_rules 9): -20. More than it would cost them to go round: they go round.
- a hungry year, a ruined harvest, a party with nothing: -10 to -30, and they say so.
Clamp 5 to 95. On a failure, what they do depends on who is stronger and who they are:
- Weaker than the bar: haggle (they offer about half), plead, or turn back and go round.
- About equal: argue, cite the Peace, demand to see the deed, threaten to complain to their patrol, or wait on the bank for another party to make a bigger one.
- Stronger than the bar: drive through. They do not come to kill; they push the bar aside and go. It becomes a fight only if they are physically stopped.
- ON BUSINESS parties argue office first, whatever the strength.
- Going round costs a loaded ox cart 4 to 5 days by the other bridges (about 40 km out, 10_world 2): wages, feed and risk worth far more than 5 coins. Everyone on the road can do that sum, which is why a fair toll gets paid once it cannot be dodged.
- This roll decides the first response only. What follows is played: people can be talked round, frightened, shamed or offended, and they remember.
- The player sets the toll, the orders at the bar and what "no" is answered with. Nothing is collected, waived or enforced automatically beyond the standing orders he has given.

6 WORD: what the roads say about the crossing
- UNKNOWN (Day 1): a healer bought a ruin. Nobody expects a toll.
- RUMOUR: something is at the bridges. Some say dead men.
- KNOWN: there is a toll, the guards are dead, and those who paid crossed safely.
- ESTABLISHED: convoy masters budget for it and plan their camps by it.
- Word moves only through play: by who crossed, what they saw, what happened to those who refused, and what Briar Tolliver's carters tell each other. It can go down as well as up, and it has a second face (fair or feared) that is narrative only. Current word: STATUS.
- News reaches the nearest towns at travel speed: 2 days to Karrow Gate and Aldermere, 3 to Tollwick, half that for a rider (10_world 5, 6).

7 THE DEAD OF THE ROAD
- The vale has monsters, monster peoples and no law. Once a week roll d100 for a fight or death within a few kilometres of the land that Mercy had no hand in: 15 or less from December to April · 25 or less from May to August · 40 or less in September and October · 25 or less in November.
- If so: d6 dead, of whichever sides fought (guards, carters, robbers, goblins, beasts). He hears of it only if someone tells him, a minion is posted to see, or the survivors come to the crossing.
- Who has a claim on a body (kin, an employer, the company's articles, Nerys's burial-priests) is settled in play (05m 1).
- Anyone who dies on his land, by any cause, is recorded in STATUS REMAINS with what is known of who they were.

8 THE ELEVEN
- The recurring people of 06_cast come when their own reasons bring them, not on a table. Each has a reason to come and look in the first season: the three lenders to see their security, the two captains because their roads end at his gate, the Proctor to count hulls, the burial-priest and the Speaker for the graveyard, the Convoy-Mistress before Roads' Opening (23 April), the Captain when a convoy hires her, the Quartermaster by proxy first.
- At the first play chat, roll each one's first visit hidden as D(2 + d30), except Briar Tolliver: before D30. Keep the days in STATUS GM. A visit can be brought forward by news (section 6) and is never cancelled by convenience.

9 WHAT THE CROSSING COULD TAKE
- If every party in an ordinary year paid the toll card: about 19,700 coins by road and 5,100 by river, about 24,800 in all; more than half of it in September and October. This is arithmetic from the tables above, not a promise.
- Against it: interest of 9,120 a year from 25 March 755 (10_world 4).
