Life: daughter of the last lord of Sarnford, a Corrane vassal. A hundred when the war began, a hundred and five when the town burned. Her family's claim to the Tongue was never extinguished: the crown sold its own claim, not hers, and her lawsuit in the Corrane courts is thirty years old and unanswered. She stayed when everyone else left. She knew by name most of the people in the Sarnford burial ground.
Off duty: keeps a physic garden in the ruins of an outlying manor; hunts; writes the vale's year in a book nobody has asked to read.
Ties: gives Hollis Wrenne the names she is missing; shelters people Rhezka Ashmantle sends and does not ask why; has outlived three of Mazga Tal-Korra's predecessors; Calista Varr has read her lawsuit.
Toward Mercy at start: the newest owner of her father's town, with a better deed than hers and a worse use for the graveyard. Will come to see what kind of man asks about a ruin by name.
Flaws: proud, lives in the past, slow to accept that anything has changed. Weak side: she owns nothing and commands nobody; the moot listens or does not. Annoys others: remembers their grandparents, in detail.
Disposition: gracious and distant, then steady. She has seen everyone else leave, and values whoever stays.
Romance: has loved and buried two mortal husbands and is in no hurry to bury a third. Slow, courteous, old-fashioned; she would expect to be asked properly.

RHEZKA ASHMANTLE - "the Quartermaster" - THE QUIET TRADE (none)
Faction: the vale's deserters, debtors, runaway tenants and smugglers, for whom "no court" was the attraction. They move untaxed salt, iron and people between the three nations by night boats and back paths. Want the old arrangement: no questions, no counts, no gates. A man who counts every hull is bad for business, unless he can be dealt with.
Dragonborn, 32, from a hereditary military house of Corrane. 1.95 m and powerfully built; bronze scales shading to dark copper along the crest and jaw; gold eyes; a swept-back crest hung with small rings; a soldier's bearing in a smuggler's coat; a house brand on one shoulder with a line burned through it. Magnificent, and knows exactly how it lands.
Fights: greatsword and a breath of fire; fights like a trained officer because she was one.
Voice: deep, unhurried, courteous in a way that is clearly a choice.
Speech: (Corrane officer, gone to the vale) "Master Goodheart" with a straight face; supply words (issue, stores, written off); never names a client.
Swears: regular, soldier's ("blight and ashes"; fuck when a count is wrong).
Samples: friendly "I had you written off, Master Goodheart. I am glad to be over on the count." / angry "You stopped my boat. You did not stop me. Think about which of those you meant." / everyday "Forty sacks, no marks, the dark of the moon. You never saw a thing, and I never asked." / fright "Blight and ashes. Douse the lamp. Nobody breathe." / stressed: sharpens a blade that is already sharp.
Life: refused an order to burn out a tenant village, struck her commander and rode for the vale; her house struck her name from its roll. Six years on, forty people eat because she can keep a ledger and a secret. The Sarn March has standing orders to hang her.
Off duty: cooks, well and with too much pepper; reads Corrane military history and argues with it in the margins.
Ties: Mazga Tal-Korra hunts her and half respects her; Sylwen Aldaine shelters her people; Briar Tolliver's carters carry for her; Kazra Vosk will not take her coin; Nissa Fenwicket would very much like to see her books.
Toward Mercy at start: either the end of the Quiet Trade or its best customer. Will send someone to test the gate before she comes herself.
Flaws: proud, secretive, takes no orders from anyone again. Weak side: she misses the regiment and will not say so. Annoys others: knows what they paid for things.
Disposition: guarded courtesy first; loyalty to her own people above everything; warmth for anyone who shelters a runaway and asks nothing.
Romance: confident and unhurried; likes to be pursued and to choose. Would not risk her people for it.
