07_NECROPOLIS CINDERFORD v1 - DRAFT (planning 23 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Reading advances no time, grants nothing. Replaces 07_buildings (the inn).
Every building, number and effect here is a narrator proposal until approved, except where marked (player ruling).

1 RULES
- Two kinds of building. CORE WORKS (section 2) need no rank: only hands, materials and time. DWELLINGS (section 3) belong to one subject each: level 1 opens at subject rank Novice, level 2 at Beginner, level 3 at Apprentice, and each later rank opens the next level. Levels are built in order.
- Caps, strength and raising come from rank alone (05m_minions). A dwelling never changes a cap. It changes what that type can do here.
- Minions and hired living workers both build (player ruling). Nobody builds on command who has a will of their own, and nothing is begun or bought automatically. Costs are quoted before commitment.
- COSTS: LD = one adult working one day. Skeletons give 2 unskilled LD a day each; gravewrights give 2 skilled LD a day each under direction (05m). A hired labourer costs 2 to 3 coins a day, a craftsman 5 to 8 (10_world 7). "skilled" needs a craftsman or a directed gravewright, or it takes double.
- Timber load = 1 LD to fell and drag. Thatch load = 1 LD from the river meadows. Stone = 2 loads per LD from the ruins of Sarnford (about 800 loads there; after that, quarried on the north rim at 1 load per LD plus haulage). Iron and bought goods in coins (c).
- Frozen ground (about December to February) stops digging, mortar and clay work. Repair after damage costs a share of the original.
- Effects change what is possible or what a success achieves, never a chance, unless the line says so.
- Levels written in full: core works levels 1 to 3; dwellings up to Apprentice. Later levels are named only and are costed when needed.
- Where things stand: the Tongue is for Mercy and his own. Works marked BANK stand on his bank land and are open to the living.
- As found on Day 1 (10_world 2): two stone bridges in poor repair (carts cross one at a time; heavy wagons should not); a neck wall breached in three places with a gateway and no gate; a roofless toll-house shell; a choked well; a burial ground. No roof.

2 CORE WORKS

1 THE TOWER
- Level 1 · Toll-house Reroofed: 20 LD skilled · 8 timber · 6 thatch · iron 15c. One dry room with a door that locks. A night's sleep here is a night's sleep.
- Level 2 · Tower Stump: 250 LD skilled · 120 stone · 30 timber · lime and iron 80c. Two storeys of stone on the highest ground. A strongroom. Sees both bridges and the neck gate.
- Level 3 · The Tower: 500 LD skilled · 260 stone · 60 timber · lime, lead and iron 250c. Four storeys. Sees 5 km of each road and river by day. Seen from as far. Only siege engines or magic break in.
- Later levels: the big bad tower; a spire; an undercroft.
- Add-ons: Well cleared (10 LD · 2 stone · rope and bucket 4c). Signal brazier on the roof (2 LD · iron 10c).
2 THE NECK WALL
- Level 1 · Breaches Stopped: 60 LD · 40 stone · 10 timber. The three breaches closed with dry stone and stakes; a barred timber gate. Keeps out beasts and anyone not ready to climb.
- Level 2 · Wall Made Sound: 300 LD skilled · 200 stone · lime 40c. 4 m high with a wall walk, river to river. A dozen defenders hold it against Danger 25 raiders.
- Level 3 · Gatehouse: 150 LD skilled · 60 stone · 30 timber · iron 80c. The Iron Road passes under it. The gate holds a rush for several exchanges. Toll is taken under cover.
- Later levels: flanking towers; a ditch cut from river to river, which makes the Tongue an island at need; a drawbridge.
- Add-ons: Gate bell (5c). Toll board with the rates painted (2 LD · paint 5c).
3 THE BRIDGES (each level is built once for the North Bridge and once for the South Bridge)
- Level 1 · Made Safe: 40 LD skilled · 15 stone · 10 timber · lime 10c. Heavy wagons cross. Carts pass both ways at once.
- Level 2 · Toll Bar: 15 LD · 6 timber · iron 20c. A bar and a booth at the Tongue end. Nobody crosses uncounted.
- Level 3 · Bridge Gate: 120 LD skilled · 50 stone · 10 timber · iron 60c. A gate tower on the bridge. Four defenders hold it against Danger 25.
- Later levels: a drawspan; a second arch widened for barges.
- Add-ons: Lantern at each end (6c). Parapet rebuilt (10 LD · 6 stone).
4 RIVER WORKS
- Level 1 · Landing: 30 LD · 20 timber. A staithe at the tip of the Tongue. Boats tie up and are counted and tolled.
- Level 2 · Chain Booms: 80 LD skilled · 20 timber · two chains 800c. One chain across the mouth of the Upper Sarn and one across the mouth of the Corrow, raised and lowered from the tip. No boat passes between the three rivers while they are up. Worked by ferry-wights from Apprentice, or by eight living hands.
- Level 3 · Quay: 200 LD skilled · 80 stone · 40 timber · iron 40c. Barges unload. Cargo can be held against an unpaid toll.
- Later levels: a crane; a boom tower; a dry dock.
- Add-ons: Depth posts (4 LD · 2 timber). Mooring lantern (6c).
5 THE CHARNEL
- Level 1 · Bone Store: 10 LD · 4 timber · 2 thatch. Sorted remains for 100 kept dry and counted.
- Level 2 · Raising Slab: 30 LD skilled · 12 stone · 20c · needs the Bone Store. A rite done on the slab takes half its time.
- Level 3 · Ossuary: 120 LD skilled · 70 stone · lime 20c. Remains for 1,000. A rite done on the slab costs three quarters of its mana, rounded up.
- Later levels: amplifier; catacombs under the Tongue.
- Add-ons: Salt pit (4 LD · salt 15c): a fleshed corpse keeps three months. Name book (paper and ink 8c).
6 THE CRYSTAL VAULT (player ruling: one only, ever)
- One level · Crystal Vault: 150 LD skilled · 60 stone · 10 timber · glass, silver and salts 600c · needs a lich. A dark, dry cellar with a growing bed.
- Grows one resurrection crystal a month, provided that every day of the month either one lich gave it all her mana, or all the liches gave it a part of theirs (player ruling). A missed day adds a day.
- Only Mercy can use a crystal grown here. They are not sold (player ruling).
7 BANKSIDE (BANK; for the living)
- Level 1 · Camp Grounds: 20 LD each bank · 2 timber. Cleared pitches, a latrine downwind, a watering place. Travellers camp, 1 coin a night.
- Level 2 · Market Pitch: 35 LD · 12 timber · 10 stone · scales and weights 50c. Six stall frames and a weigh-beam. Stall fees.
- Level 3 · Bank Row: 200 LD skilled · 90 timber · 40 thatch · iron 60c. Plots and frames for six houses the living may rent.
- Later levels: an inn someone else keeps; a chapel, if anyone will build one here.
- Add-ons: Notice board (1 LD). Hitching rails and trough (2 LD · 2 timber).

3 DWELLINGS

== SKELETONS (Linux/Bash) · CURSED TEMPLE ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Bone Racks: 8 LD · 4 timber · 1 thatch. Eight skeletons are raised in one rite of 30 minutes (mana unchanged).
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Tool Store: 15 LD · 6 timber · tools 60c. Picks, spades, axes and barrows for four squads. Squads fell timber and dig ditches without anyone standing over them.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Cursed Temple: 80 LD skilled · 30 stone · 15 timber · shields and spears 200c. Arms for 64. A destroyed skeleton rises here.
- Later levels: drill yard; barracks vaults.
== STITCHED DEAD (Git/Gerrit) · GRAVEYARD ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Stitching Table: 5 LD · 2 timber · needles, thread and salt 10c · needs a roof. Stitched dead are raised in half the time.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Salvage Shed: 20 LD · 8 timber · 3 thatch. Kit from the fallen is mended and stored. With kit in store, the goods cost of a barrow guard or bone archer is halved.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Graveyard: 60 LD · 20 stone · 10 timber. Walled and ordered. Remains the stitched bring in are sorted the same night.
- Later levels: flesh-works; a colossus frame.
== DEATH KNIGHTS (Python) · HALL OF DARKNESS ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Arming Post: 10 LD · 4 timber · iron 10c · needs a roof. A harness stand and a whetstone. A knight's kit stays sound without a smith.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Armoury: 40 LD skilled · 15 timber · 10 stone · 80c. Scavenged harness is made sound here: a death knight's goods cost is 150c, not 300c.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Hall of Darkness: 150 LD skilled · 80 stone · 30 timber · iron 60c. Where those who refuse the toll are received. A knight on the Tongue hears its bell and comes.
- Later levels: black stables wing; a lists yard.
== BARROW GUARDS (Cybersecurity) · TOMB OF SOULS ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Guard Niche: 4 LD · 6 stone each. A stone niche at one post. A guard in it cannot be taken from the side or behind.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Barrow: 60 LD · 40 stone. A vault under a turf mound: a strongroom nobody finds by looking.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Tomb of Souls: 120 LD skilled · 60 stone · iron door 100c. Only siege engines or magic open it.
- Later levels: a second vault; a false tomb.
== NIGHTMARES (Docker/K8s) · BLACK STABLE ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Picket Line: 3 LD · 2 timber. Tack kept dry. Living beasts are kept upwind of it.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Black Stable: 40 LD · 25 timber · 10 thatch · iron 15c · cart and harness 60c. A cart and a barge-line. Haulage on the land without oxen.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Courier Post: 20 LD skilled · 6 timber · saddlebags and seals 30c. A riderless courier runs to Karrow Gate, Aldermere and Tollwick once someone has ridden each road. Letters go out and come back.
- Later levels: remount posts on each road; a carriage house.
== GRAVEWRIGHTS (IaC) · MASONS' LODGE ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Tracing Floor: 6 LD · 3 timber · plaster 5c · needs a roof. A plan drawn here directs the gravewrights without Mercy standing by.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Lodge: 50 LD skilled · 20 timber · 10 stone · 8 thatch · 40c. A sawpit and a lime kiln. Boards and lime are made on site, not bought.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Yard and Crane: 60 LD skilled · 25 timber · rope and iron 80c. Stone is lifted above two storeys.
- Later levels: quarry on the north rim; engine yard.
== VAMPIRES (Azure) · ESTATE ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Toll Office: 15 LD skilled · 6 timber · 2 thatch · desk, ledgers and lamp 30c. A shuttered room at a gate. A vampire clerks there by day.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Bank House (BANK): 80 LD skilled · 35 timber · 15 thatch · furnishings 150c. A proper room to receive the living: bankers, envoys, convoy masters. Talks happen under a roof and off the Tongue.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Estate: 200 LD skilled · 80 stone · 40 timber · furnishings 400c. Guest chambers. An embassy can stay.
- Later levels: a great hall; a cellar nobody asks about.
== LICHES (AI) · MAUSOLEUM ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Book Room: 12 LD skilled · 4 timber · paper and ink 30c · needs a roof. Toll, debt, corpse and minion books kept. Nothing counted on the land goes unrecorded.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Library: 50 LD skilled · 15 timber · 10 stone · books 200c. A lich can answer questions on the law and history of the three nations (hidden check).
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Mausoleum: 180 LD skilled · 90 stone · 300c. A scrying pool: a lich's daily scrying may be done twice.
- Later levels: archive; observatory.
== BONE ARCHERS (CI/CD) · WATCH SPIRES ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Watch Platform: 10 LD · 8 timber each. 6 m of timber: the height an archer watches from.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Signal Posts: 5 LD · 2 timber · horn 4c each. At the bridges, the gate and the banks. An alarm reaches the whole land in a minute.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Bowyer's Loft: 30 LD skilled · 10 timber · 60c. Bows and arrows made on site: a bone archer's goods cost is nothing.
- Later levels: stone spires; a chain of posts to the vale's edge.
== FERRY-WIGHTS (Gravesong) · DROWNED LANDING ==
- Level 1 (Novice) · Boat Shed and Punt: 20 LD skilled · 10 timber · 30c. One flat boat. People and small loads cross in ordinary water.
- Level 2 (Beginner) · Rope Ferry: 40 LD skilled · 14 timber · rope and iron 40c. A cart ferry on a hauling line, for when a bridge is shut or broken.
- Level 3 (Apprentice) · Patrol Boats: 60 LD skilled · 30 timber · 80c. Three boats. Any boat at the tip can be met and inspected on the water.
- Later levels: a toll barge; a river gate.
