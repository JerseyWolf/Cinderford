09H_HEARTH_SPELLS CINDERFORD v1 - DRAFT (planning 23 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC. No tables. Replaces 09h_hearth_effects v0 (30 spells).

1 RULES
- ONE spell per subject in Cinderford (player Q65). Ten spells. Thornwall is not touched (Q66): its 30 spells stay as they are in the ledger and in 09_spells.
- Each Cinderford spell is ANCHORED to one Thornwall spell of the same subject. The anchor supplies the real coding task, the code in 13_spell_code and the STEP. Upgrading the anchor in a dream session upgrades the Cinderford spell, and the upgrade is already in the ledger when it goes back to Thornwall.
- The subject's other two Thornwall spells are not used in Cinderford. They may still be upgraded in dream sessions for Thornwall's sake; that changes nothing here.
- Unlock and cap as 09_spells 2: nothing at subject rank Novice; Beginner rank opens Basic to Beginner; each later rank opens one more step. A promotion only opens the cap: the spell must be upgraded by hand.
- Cost and speed as 09_spells 3: Basic 10 mana, slow, -20 · Novice 8, main action, -10 · Beginner 7, 0 · Apprentice 5, quick, +5 · Journeyman 4, +10 · Expert 3, +15 · Master 2, +22 · Grandmaster 1, +30.
- Each step includes everything the steps below it do, unless it replaces a number.
- Incantation: heard in the crackle by everyone in the hearth ring, word for word, at every cast. Through an awakened ember it is heard from the ember.
- Only Mend heals flesh.

2 THE TEN SPELLS

### FIRE RING
- Fire skill (subject): Flame (Python)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Read the Fight
- Incantation: "None shall cross my light."
- Basic: A ring of low flame 3 m across round the pit for one minute. Crossing it harms like a Danger 10 foe. Beasts will not cross.
- Novice: 5 m across, ten minutes. Beasts and frightened people flee from it.
- Beginner: Any size up to act range, one hour. Harm as Danger 25. Named friends may pass through a gap.
- Apprentice: Until dawn. Can be shaped: a line, an arc, a ring round one building.
- Journeyman: Harm as Danger 40. Burns only what the fire chooses: thatch, fences and friends are safe.
- Expert: Two rings at once, or one round an awakened ember. Lasts a full day.
- Master: Harm as Danger 60. May be laid anywhere inside sense range.
- Grandmaster: A standing ring round the whole site for a week, unseen until crossed, burning only those the fire names.
- Notes: Below Journeyman the ring is real fire and can set dry thatch and grass alight, the fire's own buildings included.

### WISP CREW
- Fire skill (subject): Ground (Linux/Bash)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Night Shift
- Incantation: "Small flames, to work."
- Basic: One wisp for 12 hours inside act range: 1 labour-day of light unskilled hauling (deadwood, water, stones; 2 kg a trip).
- Novice: One wisp for a day and a night: 2 labour-days of light work, and it feeds the fire (no fuel starvation while it lasts).
- Beginner: Two wisps for a day: 4 labour-days. Together they carry 10 kg. They work anywhere inside sense range.
- Apprentice: Three wisps: 6 labour-days. They can help a craftsman (hold, fetch, stir, turn), and one may stand watch instead and raise the alarm.
- Journeyman: Five wisps for 3 days: 10 labour-days a day. They reform if scattered.
- Expert: Wisps use tools (dig, saw, thatch) and count as full unskilled labourers. One may hold a door: Danger 10 foes must break through first.
- Master: A standing crew of 8 for a week. Shown a skilled task once, they can repeat it.
- Grandmaster: A dozen wisps bound to the site for good. Each night they can hold against Danger 25 for one exchange.
- Notes: A wisp does only work. It is not a free action of the fire and cannot fight below Expert. Labour-days count toward 07_buildings jobs.

### MEND
- Fire skill (subject): Mending (Git/Gerrit)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Revert
- Incantation: "Be whole by my warmth."
- Basic: Eases one person inside act range: the -5 of one Bruised wound is lifted for a day, or the worst of a mild fever for a night.
- Novice: Turns one Bruised wound into a scratch, or cures a common sickness (cold, flux, mild fever) overnight. Once per person per day.
- Beginner: Undoes one Bruised fully, or cures a serious fever or a festering wound. From this step it can also restore one health level of the fire itself, once per day.
- Apprentice: Hurt becomes Bruised. Or: one sickness cured in everyone sleeping inside act range tonight.
- Journeyman: Two levels on one person, or one level each on up to three. Stops an outbreak on the site.
- Expert: Badly hurt becomes Hurt. Poison is burnt out. Twice per person per day.
- Master: Down becomes Badly hurt, once per day: the dying pulled back. Plague-grade sickness cured in one person.
- Grandmaster: Any wound short of death is undone one level per cast with no daily limit. Sickness cannot take hold in anyone who lives at the site.
- Notes: Healing spell. No Mark is needed in Cinderford: the target must be inside act range and must have slept at least one night by the fire. Strangers can only be eased at the Basic step.

### BLIND EYE
- Fire skill (subject): Vigil/Veil (Cybersecurity)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Whisper Lock
- Incantation: "Look elsewhere."
- Basic: One small object inside act range (a purse, a letter, a knife) goes unnoticed by anyone not touching it, until sunset.
- Novice: One person sitting still by the fire, or one pack or chest, for a night. Searchers' eyes slide off. Ends if the person speaks or moves.
- Beginner: One person who may move and speak quietly inside act range, or one cart, for a day. A searcher who already suspects needs a Hard check.
- Apprentice: Up to three people, or the inside of one building, for 3 days. The fire feels each search.
- Journeyman: Also hides from magic: scrying, tracking spells, a Called's sight. Up to six people or a string of carts.
- Expert: The hidden may leave act range and stay hidden inside sense range. Tracks and scent are hidden too. Lasts a week.
- Master: One building, or the whole camp, looks derelict to the eyes the fire chooses.
- Grandmaster: People the fire names cannot find the site at all. A hidden person carrying an ember stays hidden wherever they go.
- Notes: Hides; does not protect. A hidden person who is stumbled into is found. Hiding someone from a nation's officers has consequences if it comes out.

### CARRIED EMBER
- Fire skill (subject): Embers (Docker/K8s)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Bottled Hearth
- Incantation: "Carry me, and I am there."
- Basic: Awakens one carried ember for an hour: the fire sees and hears clearly 10 m around it, as if it were there.
- Novice: For a day. The fire can flare, dim and give signs through it.
- Beginner: The fire can cast Basic-step spells through it. Act range 2 m around the ember.
- Apprentice: For 3 days. Act range 5 m. Native powers work through it.
- Journeyman: Two awakened embers at once. Any known spell may be cast through them at its own step.
- Expert: Act range 10 m around each. Water and smothering cannot put an awakened ember out.
- Master: Three at once. If the pit is lost the fire lives on in an awakened ember for a week and can be rekindled from it.
- Grandmaster: Every ember the fire has ever given can be awakened at will, anywhere.
- Notes: A plain carried ember (05h, Embers) only senses dimly. Awakening is what this spell adds. Someone must carry or keep the ember; the fire cannot move it.

### BRACE
- Fire skill (subject): Structure (IaC)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Lint Eye
- Incantation: "Hold fast."
- Basic: One small thing inside act range (a door, a beam, a wheel, a tent) holds against one hard blow or gust: one minute.
- Novice: One door, wall or roof holds for an hour against wind, weight or one person forcing it.
- Beginner: One whole building for a night: stands through a storm, ordinary fire will not catch on it, forcing a door is Hard.
- Apprentice: One building for a day against flood, fire and battering: Danger 25 attackers lose an exchange at each attempt. A half-built frame holds without props, so work goes on in bad weather.
- Journeyman: Two buildings or a run of fence, for 3 days. Breaking in needs a ram or Danger 40.
- Expert: Everything built inside act range, for a night.
- Master: For a week. Braced buildings stand in flood water. Only siege engines or magic break them.
- Grandmaster: While the fire is Low or better, nothing built inside act range burns, falls or floods.
- Notes: Holds what is there; repairs nothing. A braced door still opens for whoever has the right to open it.

### SANCTUARY
- Fire skill (subject): Presence (Azure)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Budget Bell
- Incantation: "This one is under my roof."
- Basic: One named person sitting inside act range, for an hour: hands laid on them to drag them away lose their grip (a Hard check to hold on). Does not stop a blade.
- Novice: One person, for a night. Blows aimed at them are Hard as well.
- Beginner: Up to three people, for a night. Anyone who means them harm feels dread and must pass a check to try at all.
- Apprentice: Everyone who has eaten at the fire tonight, inside act range, until dawn.
- Journeyman: Named people for 3 days. Warrants, writs and compelling magic fail against them here too.
- Expert: Everyone inside act range. Whoever breaks it is marked: all who meet them know they broke sanctuary.
- Master: Sanctuary goes with one named person who carries an ember.
- Grandmaster: While the fire is Low or better, no one who has eaten at the fire can be harmed or taken by force inside act range.
- Notes: Protects against force, not against persuasion, hunger or the law waiting outside. Sheltering a wanted person is a choice the neighbours will hear about.

### FAMILIAR BOND
- Fire skill (subject): Insight (AI)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Familiar Bond
- Incantation: "Out of the smoke, to me." (summon)
- Basic: A wisp of Gary: scouts and fetches, cannot harm, any hit sends him away, carries nothing, ranges about 30 m.
- Novice: Small-animal strength: hurts only Danger 10 foes; one harmful hit at Danger 10+ sends him away; carries 1 kg; about 100 m.
- Beginner: Hound-scale: hurts foes up to Danger 25; shrugs off one Bruised a day; carries 5 kg; about 300 m.
- Apprentice: Wolf-scale: hurts foes up to Danger 40; once per exchange one foe takes -10 against a defender; about 1 km.
- Journeyman: Great-owl scale: hurts foes up to Danger 60; shrugs off one Hurt a day; lifts a child or a pack; about 3 km.
- Expert: Shares his senses with the fire; can carry an ember; about 10 km.
- Master: Splits into two bodies; ignores Bruised; fights two foes in one exchange.
- Grandmaster: Ranges the whole vale, and the dead answer him.
- Notes: Gary crosses as he is. The Bond was cast in Thornwall and is never recast. Summon cost: half the step cost, rounded up. Skill follows AI rank. Sent away by harm until the next dawn. Cannot die.

### SPARK VOLLEY
- Fire skill (subject): Sparks (CI/CD)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Twelve Looms
- Incantation: "Let the sparks fall like rain."
- Basic: Two sparks at one target inside act range; harm counts once. Hurts Danger 10 foes.
- Novice: Two targets inside act range, both at Hard; all must succeed.
- Beginner: Three targets, out to twice act range, at Hard; all must succeed.
- Apprentice: Four targets anywhere inside sense range. Hurts foes up to Danger 25. Friends and thatch are never hit.
- Journeyman: Six targets, each rolled separately so one miss does not spoil the rest.
- Expert: Eight targets at Normal instead of Hard. Hurts foes up to Danger 40.
- Master: Twelve targets. Hurts foes up to Danger 60. A struck foe can be pinned to cover (loses its next main action).
- Grandmaster: Twelve at Normal; each hit also staggers (-10 on that target's next action).
- Notes: Below Apprentice a miss lands somewhere: sparks can set dry thatch and grass alight.

### FOLLOW
- Fire skill (subject): Roads (Gravesong)
- Anchor (Thornwall spell, shared step and code): Haunt
- Incantation: "My light goes with you."
- Basic: For an hour: the rough direction of one person who sat at the fire today, while they are inside sense range or on a road within its doubled reach.
- Novice: Until sunset: direction, out to 1 km along a road.
- Beginner: Until the next dawn: direction and rough distance out to 5 km, and whether they are moving, still or asleep.
- Apprentice: For 3 days, to the edges of the vale (about 15 km). Whether they are hurt or afraid. Two people at once.
- Journeyman: For a week, as far as the nearest towns (about 50 km). How many are with them, and the moment they turn for home.
- Expert: Once a day a glimpse of where they are. The fire can send one feeling: come back, danger, all is well.
- Master: For a month, any distance along the three roads. A minute of sight and hearing each day. Three people at once.
- Grandmaster: Everyone who has slept at the fire, always. Once a night, one sentence in a voice they know.
- Notes: For settlers away, and for those who robbed the house. From Beginner the fire may instead make it a haunting: the target sleeps badly and takes -5 on all checks next day.

- Mend: Basic (Revert Basic) · 10 mana.
- Carried Ember: Basic (Bottled Hearth Basic) · 10 mana.
- Familiar Bond: Novice · summon 4 mana.
- Locked until the subject reaches Beginner: Fire Ring (Python), Blind Eye (Cybersecurity), Brace (IaC), Sanctuary (Azure), Spark Volley (CI/CD), Follow (Gravesong).
