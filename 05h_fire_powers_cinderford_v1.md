1 WHAT A RANK GIVES IN CINDERFORD
A subject's full rank in the ledger gives, at once and in every way listed:
- CHECK BASE: the fire skill rolls at the rank base (20/30/45/60/75/85/95).
- NATIVE POWERS: what the fire can do with that fire skill WITHOUT spending mana (section 3). Using a native power for something uncertain is a check. Native powers act inside act range unless the line says otherwise.
- BUILDINGS: building 1 of that skill at Novice, building 2 at Beginner, building 3 at Apprentice; each later rank opens the next level of each (07_buildings).
- SPELL CAP: the subject's ONE Cinderford spell (09h_hearth_spells) can be upgraded up to the step of that rank. Novice rank: that spell is locked.
- +3 MAX MANA and RANGE growth per promotion (02_rules_cinderford 7.1).
Powers cumulate: each rank includes all lower ones.

2 SET BY THE HIGHEST RANK YOU HOLD (Q64)
SPEECH:
- Novice: signs (flame height, colour, heat, crackle, smoke).
- Beginner: faint dreams: one image or feeling to one sleeper inside act range per night. AVAILABLE NOW (highest rank Beginner).
- Apprentice: clear dreams: a short scene and a few words to one sleeper per night.
- Journeyman: voice in the crackle to one chosen person inside act range.
- Expert: open voice to everyone at the hearth.
- Master: voice through carried embers and outposts.
- Grandmaster: voice anywhere in sense range, to anyone or everyone.
Incantations are heard at every rank (09h 2).

3 NATIVE POWERS BY FIRE SKILL

FLAME (Python) - Direct force from the pit: heat, flare, burn, shove.
- Novice (20): Flare to twice height or bank to coals at will; heat felt inside act range; can light anything dry that touches the ring.
- Beginner (30): Throw a burning brand or a gout of flame 2 m from the ring (Danger 10 harm); dry a soaked camp in an hour; boil a pot in minutes.
- Apprentice (45): Push with heat: a shove that staggers a person inside act range; set a chosen thing inside act range alight; refuse to burn (a thing laid in the fire comes out unharmed).
- Journeyman (60): Flame leaps to any point in act range (harm up to Danger 25); hold a wall of fire 5 m long for an exchange; smelt and work soft metal in the pit.
- Expert (75): Harm up to Danger 40 anywhere in act range; heat a building evenly through winter; fire inside act range that is not yours obeys you (spread, stop, go out).
- Master (85): Harm up to Danger 60; fire-shapes that hold for a minute (a hand, a hound, a door); forge-heat on demand.
- Grandmaster (95): Fire anywhere in sense range is yours to command; you can be lit again from any fire you have touched.

GROUND (Linux/Bash) - Knowing the land, weather and tracks inside sense range.
- Novice (20): Tell tomorrow's weather roughly; feel footsteps and hooves inside sense range; know the ford is rising or falling.
- Beginner (30): Weather two days ahead, reliably; read tracks inside sense range (how many, how heavy, how long ago); feel where water and good stone lie under act range.
- Apprentice (45): Weather for a week; sense digging, burrowing and things under the soil inside sense range; tell the ford's depth exactly.
- Journeyman (60): Feel every living thing bigger than a fox inside sense range, even hidden; know a storm or flood a day before it comes.
- Expert (75): Read the land's history (old foundations, graves, battles) inside sense range; know when the ground will freeze or thaw to the day.
- Master (85): Sense through a carried ember as well as at the pit; foresee a season's weather in outline.
- Grandmaster (95): Know the whole vale as a body knows its hand: every road, every camp, every grave.

MENDING (Git/Gerrit) - Warmth that heals, comforts and repairs.
- Novice (20): Warmth: people resting at the fire recover from cold and exhaustion at the normal rate even in foul weather.
- Beginner (30): Healing warmth: a Bruised person who sleeps inside act range heals in half the time; nobody inside act range dies of cold.
- Apprentice (45): Fevers break, wounds do not fester, and Hurt heals in half the time inside act range.
- Journeyman (60): Knit small things: a cracked pot, a split board, a torn sail made whole overnight in the warmth.
- Expert (75): Badly hurt can recover without a healer inside act range; slow poisons are burnt out by a night by the fire.
- Master (85): Repair a broken building part overnight; a dying person kept warm by the fire lives until morning.
- Grandmaster (95): Warmth undoes almost any harm given time; the fire's own health returns one level every night.

VIGIL / VEIL (Cybersecurity) - Noticing threats; hiding what you are.
- Novice (20): Vigil: you never miss someone entering act range. Veil: you pass for an ordinary fire to anyone not testing you.
- Beginner (30): Vigil: feel hostility (held weapon, quickened breath) inside act range. Veil: an incantation can be passed off as the wood crackling (check).
- Apprentice (45): Vigil: feel intent inside sense range (someone circling, someone waiting). Veil: you can go out-looking (cold ash) for a night and come back.
- Journeyman (60): Vigil: know if someone at the fire is carrying poison, a hidden blade or a forged seal. Veil: Called and mages cannot tell you are more than a fire unless they test with care.
- Expert (75): Vigil: foresight of an attack one exchange early, anywhere in act range. Veil: you can hide one building or person from casual eyes inside act range.
- Master (85): Vigil: nothing approaches the site unseen. Veil: the whole site can look abandoned to strangers for a night.
- Grandmaster (95): Vigil and Veil both cover the vale; you choose who finds the crossroads.

EMBERS (Docker/K8s) - Plain embers carried away, and beasts. (Seeing clearly, acting or casting through an ember is the Carried Ember spell.)
- Novice (20): one plain ember can be carried in a pot or lantern for a day; the fire feels dimly whether it is warm, moving or in danger.
- Beginner (30): one ember for 3 days; dim sense of shapes and voices within 5 m of it; beasts tied inside act range stay calm in storms and near wolves.
- Apprentice (45): two embers for a week; lost beasts that know the fire find their way to it.
- Journeyman (60): three embers; an ember set in another hearth keeps as long as that hearth is fed; lame horses and oxen mend at the fire.
- Expert (75): five embers; any beast that has wintered at the fire will not attack its people.
- Master (85): embers keep a month unfed; the fire knows at once when one is put out, and where.
- Grandmaster (95): any hearth that has taken an ember stays kindled while the pit burns.

STRUCTURE (IaC) - Telekinesis and building work.
- Novice (20): Move a twig, a coal, a pot lid inside act range; keep the ring stones and a fallen log in place.
- Beginner (30): Lift and move up to 5 kg inside act range at walking pace; one LD of light work per day on a site job (carrying, stacking).
- Apprentice (45): Up to 25 kg; 2 LD per day; hold a beam or door shut against one person's strength.
- Journeyman (60): Up to 100 kg; 4 LD per day, including skilled-help work under a craftsman; hurl small objects (Danger 10 harm).
- Expert (75): Up to 300 kg; 8 LD per day; raise a frame or wall section alone; hold a door against a crowd.
- Master (85): Up to a tonne; one cart-sized thing anywhere in act range.
- Grandmaster (95): Anything in act range that is not rooted moves at your will; a building can be raised in days.

PRESENCE (Azure) - Aura, calm, sway; how people feel at the fire.
- Novice (20): Calm: people at the fire sleep well and quarrel less. Offerings: you can taste their worth.
- Beginner (30): Comfort: frightened people at the fire settle; a guest who means harm feels uneasy (not stopped).
- Apprentice (45): Sway: one person at the fire is nudged toward a mood (trust, wariness, generosity); they can resist. Strangers are drawn to stop (+5 arrival roll).
- Journeyman (60): Guest-peace: anyone who has eaten at the fire feels the breaking of guest-right as a real dread; the house's name travels well (+5 arrival roll more).
- Expert (75): Awe: the whole hearth ring can be stilled for a moment; envoys and priests treat the place as a power.
- Master (85): A gathering at the fire can be held to a truce for one night against anything short of open hatred.
- Grandmaster (95): Whoever sits at your fire is safe from violence there until morning, and everyone in three nations knows it.

INSIGHT (AI) - Reading people; the familiar's skill.
- Novice (20): Read mood from voice and posture inside act range like a watchful person would.
- Beginner (30): Notice a lie of the obvious kind; know who in a group defers to whom. Gary sees and fetches for you (summon).
- Apprentice (45): Know what someone at the fire wants tonight; tell a practised lie from the truth on a check.
- Journeyman (60): Know someone's fear after an evening at the fire; Gary can watch someone through a night out of range and report as images.
- Expert (75): Know what someone is hiding in general terms; Gary shares senses with you.
- Master (85): Understand a whole household's web of loyalties after a week.
- Grandmaster (95): Nobody who has sat at the fire for a night is a stranger to you.

SPARKS (CI/CD) - Reaching out: light at a distance, ranged strike.
- Novice (20): Throw a spark to light a lantern, a candle or a torch inside act range.
- Beginner (30): A visible light at any point in sense range for a minute; a stinging spark at one target in act range (scratch harm, scares beasts).
- Apprentice (45): A spark that harms at sense range (Danger 10); light a signal fire you can see.
- Journeyman (60): Sparks harm up to Danger 25 at sense range; a light can follow a person along a road.
- Expert (75): Up to Danger 40; several targets; your beacon can carry a simple agreed message.
- Master (85): Up to Danger 60; a light anywhere along the roads within the vale.
- Grandmaster (95): Strike anything you can sense.

ROADS (Gravesong) - Reach along roads, links between embers, the dead.
- Novice (20): Sense along the roads twice as far as elsewhere (already in 02_rules 7.1). Feel the road's traffic as a pulse.
- Beginner (30): Know which road someone came by and roughly how far they walked today; feel the dead in the ground inside sense range as cold places.
- Apprentice (45): Sense along the roads three times as far; feel a regular's approach a day out.
- Journeyman (60): Ember outposts can see each other through you; know when one of the dead in sense range is restless.
- Expert (75): Sense along roads to the vale's edge; restless dead inside sense range can be quieted by warmth.
- Master (85): Hear what is said beside any waystone or ember on the roads.
- Grandmaster (95): The roads of the vale are part of you; news reaches you before it reaches the towns.

5 LIMITS
- A native power does exactly its line. Anything larger is a spell or not possible yet.
- Harm values are the Danger tier of foe it can hurt, as for Gary (09_spells Familiar Bond).
- Native powers cost no mana but tire: using one hard for more than an hour is -10 on the next check with that fire skill until the next dawn.
- Health levels limit powers as they limit range (02_rules_cinderford 8). At Embers, none work.
