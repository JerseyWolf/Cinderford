08_ARRIVALS CINDERFORD v0 - DRAFT (planning 23 Nov 2026; not canon until the player approves). PUBLIC: how arrivals work, not who is coming. No tables. Reading advances no time.

1 PRINCIPLE
There are no quest lines. The road brings whoever the season, the weather and the news bring. Everyone who arrives was somewhere before and is going somewhere after, for a reason of their own. Most are ordinary. Most do not notice the fire as anything but a fire.

2 THE DAILY ROLL
- Once per day of play the narrator makes a hidden d100 roll for how many parties STOP at the crossroads that day (many more pass without stopping).
- The season sets the odds (10_world 5): almost nobody in the thaw and deep winter, a few a day in summer, convoys in autumn.
- Pushing the roll up: a roof; food for sale; stabling; a good name on the road; foul weather that makes people shelter.
- Pushing it down: the ford in flood; storms; snow; a bad name; fresh news of robbery or sickness; soldiers nearby.
- Skipped time is rolled day by day all the same, then told briefly.

3 WHO THEY ARE
- For each party the narrator rolls hidden for: what kind of travellers; which road they came by and where they are from; how many; what they want tonight; what trouble they carry; their temper; how long they mean to stay.
- Kinds include vale folk, pedlars, carters, drovers, pilgrims and priests, riders and officials, soldiers and hired guards, fugitives, adventurers, monster folk, robbers, scholars, and now and then something strange.
- Monster folk are travellers too. Some trade, some beg, some steal, some attack. Hunger and season matter more than kind.

4 CONTINUITY
- People come back. A carter who went north on Monday comes south again when the distance allows. Regulars are tracked from their first visit.
- People talk. What happens at the fire is told in Karrow Gate, Aldermere and Tollwick within days, in the teller's version.
- People remember. Kindness, cheating, a broken guest-right and a strange light in the crackle all travel.

5 WHO MIGHT STAY
- A few arrivals are written in depth from the start. The fire cannot tell which.
- Staying is decided as 06_settlers 1 describes. Nobody settles because a roll said so.

6 WHAT THE FIRE SEES
- Inside sense range (now 50 m; 100 m along the roads): who comes, how they move, what they carry openly.
- Inside act range (now 10 m): faces, words spoken at ordinary voice, what is laid down by the fire.
- Anything more is a check (Vigil, Insight, Roads) or a spell.

7 EARLY DAYS
From Day 1 expect very little: thaw, high water, empty roads. That is intended (player ruling: slow and empty at first).
